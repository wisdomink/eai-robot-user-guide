# 3.6 Robot Interaction Procedures Guide

## 3.6.1 Robot Interaction Precautions

1. During voice-controlled walking or upper-limb motion commands, keep at least a 1-meter radius clear around the robot.
2. During voice-controlled walking or motion execution, pay close attention to robot safety to avoid falling.
3. It is recommended to perform motion commands on hard flooring instead of soft surfaces such as grass or carpets.
4. For customized speech corpus configuration, contact FF EAI Robotic technical support.

## 3.6.2 Current Version (External Microphone)

- Only external microphone interaction is currently supported.
- Connect and pair the packaged external microphone.
- After the robot is connected to the network, turn on the microphone to activate voice interaction.
- No wake-up word is required for the external microphone version.

## 3.6.3 Upcoming Version (Built-in Microphone, OTA Update)

- The built-in microphone interaction feature will be introduced through a future OTA update.
- After the update, users can manually switch between the built-in and external microphones in the app.
- When using the built-in microphone, the robot must be activated with the wake word `Hey, Master`.

### 3.6.3.1 Voice Chat

- Everyday topics such as news, weather, and surrounding environment.
- FF Master topics including personality, preferences, and product details.
- Knowledge-base topics depending on system configuration.
- Supports multi-language interaction including English, Chinese, Spanish, French, and Italian.

### 3.6.3.2 Multimodal Interaction

- Environment and object Q&A.
- Detection of the number of people and recognition of clothing, facial expressions, or gender.
- Customized guest greeting with future face registration support.

### 3.6.3.3 Voice Command Actions and Expressions

Voice commands can trigger:

- Upper-limb and hand actions
- Head movements
- Walking actions
- Waist movements
- Expressive actions
- Facial screen expressions

### 3.6.3.4 Head Pat Interaction

Gently touch the head touch sensor. The robot will nod pleasantly and respond with: `Friendly touch signal detected — energy +100%!`

### 3.6.3.5 Robot Interaction Customization

Knowledge base customization, character profile, wake words, and voice tone selection are under development and will be supported through the future Intellectual & Interactive Platform.

## 3.6.4 External Microphone Pairing and Connection

1. Press the power button on the microphone to turn it on.
2. The external microphone receiver is integrated into the robot head, and the microphone will automatically pair with the robot.
3. A solid green light indicates successful connection.
4. If pairing fails, restart the robot. If it still fails, use the pairing hole on the back of the robot head and the microphone pairing button to complete manual pairing.
