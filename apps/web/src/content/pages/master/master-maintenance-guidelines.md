# 1.3 Maintenance and Management Guidelines

1. Regular Maintenance: Maintenance and upkeep should be carried out regularly according to the recommendations of FF EAI-Robotics. This helps keep all parts in optimal working condition and extends service life.

2. Fault Handling

- If a malfunction occurs during robot operation, immediately stop the operation and notify professional technicians for inspection and repair.
- Do not disassemble, adjust, or repair the equipment while it is powered on or in operation.

3. Battery and Power Management

- Ensure batteries are charged in a safe and dry environment.
- Avoid leaving the robot connected to a power source for extended periods to prevent overcharging or overheating.
- If the robot falls, immediately check the condition of the battery. If any damage or deformation is observed, discontinue use and contact official after-sales support.

4. Software Updates: Regularly check for and update the robot's operating system and control software to ensure optimal functionality and improved system security.
