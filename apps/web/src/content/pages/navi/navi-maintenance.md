# Maintenance and Care

## Usage Care

### Post-Operation Care

- Stop NAVI on a relatively flat surface before powering off.
- Always use the original charger to prevent battery damage or fire risk.

### Parts Requiring Regular Maintenance or Replacement

### Main Unit Maintenance

- Check that all parts of NAVI are in good condition. If in doubt, contact the after-sales service team.
- Before performing maintenance or cleaning, ensure NAVI is powered off and the power adapter is disconnected.
- When replacing accessories, use manufacturer-specified parts to avoid damage caused by incompatibility.
- The foot pads are consumable parts. Severe wear, missing corners, or detachment may cause NAVI to move or stand unstably.
- Regularly check battery level and health. When battery life noticeably decreases, contact customer service for a battery replacement.
- Store NAVI and the power adapter in a cool, dry place.

## Battery Maintenance

- Never store the battery in an environment where the temperature exceeds 45°C or falls below -10°C. Long-term storage under such conditions will degrade battery performance.
- For long-term storage (more than 3 months), store the battery at 20°C to 25°C with a relative humidity of 0 to 60% RH. The battery charge should not exceed 80%; 30% to 50% is recommended.
- During storage, periodically check the battery level. If it drops below 20%, recharge the battery. It is recommended to recharge at least once every 3 months to maintain battery activity.
- The recommended charging temperature for the battery is 10°C to 25°C.
- Do not charge the battery for more than 24 hours, as overcharging will shorten battery life.
- After under-voltage protection is triggered, the battery cannot output power; it must be charged before normal operation can resume.
- Avoid deep discharge of the battery, as this may permanently disable it and make it unable to charge again.
- The battery will automatically stop charging when fully charged. It is recommended to disconnect from the charger after fully charged.
