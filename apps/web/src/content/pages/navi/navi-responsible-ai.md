# Responsible AI & Robotics Use Guidelines

This product is designed to support learning, creativity, robotics exploration, and supervised educational use. To ensure safe and responsible use, please follow the principles below:

## Human Supervision Required
The robot and AI features are learning tools. A responsible adult, teacher, or trained operator should supervise use, especially when used by children, students, or in public environments.

## AI Transparency
Users should be clearly informed when they are interacting with AI-generated responses, AI-assisted features, or autonomous robot behaviors.

## Child Safety First
When used by minors, especially children under 13, the product should be used with parent, guardian, teacher, or institutional supervision. Age-appropriate settings, privacy controls, and safer-by-default configurations should be applied.

## Use Only for Intended Purposes
The product should only be used for approved educational, demonstration, development, or operational scenarios. It should not be used to provide medical, legal, psychological, financial, or emergency advice.

## Data Minimization
Collect and retain only the data necessary for product operation, learning progress, safety, troubleshooting, and authorized educational purposes. Avoid uploading or sharing unnecessary personal information.

## No Manipulative Use
The product should not be used to manipulate users, create emotional dependency, encourage excessive engagement, or pressure users into unwanted actions.

## Explainable and Reviewable Actions
Important AI-assisted outputs, recommendations, scores, or flags should be reviewable by a human supervisor. AI-generated content should not be treated as final without appropriate review.

## Physical Safety Boundaries
Keep a safe distance from moving parts. Use emergency stop features where available. Do not operate the robot in a way that may cause injury, collision, property damage, or unsafe interaction with people or animals.

## Fair and Respectful Use
The product should be used in a way that respects all users and avoids discriminatory, harmful, harassing, or biased interactions.

## Content and Intellectual Property Respect
Users should respect copyright, trademarks, and third-party content rights when uploading, creating, or sharing course materials, media, code, or robot projects.

## Accuracy and Limitations
AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should verify important information through trusted sources and should not rely on AI outputs for high-stakes decisions.

## Consent and Privacy
Obtain appropriate consent before recording, uploading, sharing, or processing images, voice, video, personal data, or student information.

## Human Override and Reversibility
Robot actions and AI-assisted outputs should be controllable, stoppable, and reviewable by a human operator whenever possible.

## Responsible Operation
Use the product in a safe, ethical, and environmentally responsible manner. Follow all instructions, warnings, applicable laws, school policies, and organizational rules.
