### Unboxing Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| NAVI fails to power on (button light turns on then goes off) | Incorrect startup method | Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds; release after hearing the "humming" tone to complete startup. |
| NAVI fails to power on (button light turns on then quickly goes off) | Button pressed too frequently | Wait 5 seconds, then restart following the instructions. |
| NAVI fails to power on (button light does not turn on / turns on then goes off / stays on but battery indicator does not light) | Insufficient battery | Remove the charger from the accessory box and charge NAVI for approximately 90 minutes. |
| NAVI fails to power on (button light does not turn on / turns on then goes off / stays on but battery indicator does not light) | Incorrect static posture | Lay NAVI's limbs flat, correct the posture, and restart. |

## Powering On Outside the Box

If you are starting NAVI outside the box, please observe the following pre-startup conditions.

### Hard and Level Surface
The surface should not visibly indent under pressure. Avoid starting on uneven surfaces or overly soft carpets.

### No Obvious Obstructions on the Surface
There should be no visible obstructions or protrusions on the surface contacting NAVI's legs and belly. Avoid items such as cardboard or children's toys that may affect NAVI's static posture.

### Lower Legs Folded to Structural Stop
Deviation of up to 18° is acceptable. The lower legs should be in close contact with the upper legs.

### Hip Joint Actuator Angle Requirements
The hip joint and thigh angle deviation should be within 18°. Adjust the thigh and hip joint angle so that both the foot pads and knee joints flush with the ground.
