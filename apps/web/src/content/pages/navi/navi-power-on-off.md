# Power On/Off Instructions

## Pre-Startup Posture Requirements

When powered off, FX Navi cannot autonomously adjust its joint positions. Therefore, before powering on, you must manually position FX Navi's four limbs into the standard startup posture to ensure it can stand normally after powering on.

![FX Navi Pre-Startup Posture](/images/navi/navi-startup-posture.png)

Before powering on, ensure FX Navi is in the standard startup posture shown above.

![FX Navi Power Button Location](/images/navi/navi-power-button.png)

## How to Power On

Follow these steps:

Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds. Upon hearing a "humming" tone, release the button to complete the startup. The button light will illuminate, and the battery indicator lights will flash in sequence, displaying the current battery level.

## How to Power Off

To ensure FX Navi responds flexibly and precisely to shutdown commands, FX Navi offers the following two shutdown methods. Please wait until FX Navi has completely stopped moving and safely crouched down before shutting off.

> **Note:** Do not power off while FX Navi is moving or standing. Once shutdown is triggered, FX Navi will immediately lose power, which may cause personal injury or property damage.

### Standard Shutdown

1. Press and hold the power button for 3 to 5 seconds; you will hear a long "humming" tone.
2. Release the button after the tone; FX Navi will automatically shut down once all indicator lights go off.

### Force Shutdown

> **Note:** Use this only when standard shutdown cannot be completed.

1. Press and hold the power button for more than 6 seconds.
2. Release after all indicator lights go off; FX Navi will force shut down.

## Accidental Power Button Activation

### When No Pre-Startup Preparation Is Needed

When FX Navi has crouched down due to a depleted battery, it can autonomously adjust its joint positions, so no limb adjustment is needed before powering on.
