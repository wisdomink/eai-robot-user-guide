# Charging & Indicator Lights

## Direct Wired Charging

FX Navi is equipped with a charging port for convenient direct charging. This method is especially useful when FX Navi's battery is completely depleted, and it cannot move on its own. Direct charging offers the highest efficiency among all charging methods and is easy to operate. Regardless of your environment, using the FF-provided charging kit can quickly replenish FX Navi's power for continuous efficient operation.

## Understanding Charging and Operating Status via Indicator Lights

### Indicator Light Meanings

**White Battery Indicator Lights:**
Used to display current battery level and charging progress while charging.

**Dual-Color Charging Indicator Light:**
Indicates charging status: charging, fully charged, or discharge.

> **Note:** Use the following dual-color charging indicator light colors to determine FX Navi's charging status:
> - **Yellow** — currently charging
> - **White** — fully charged
> - **Off** — charger unplugged, FX Navi operating normally
