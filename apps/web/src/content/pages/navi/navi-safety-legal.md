## Precautions

- To reduce the risk of injury, always check NAVI's operating status before use.
- Start NAVI on flat ground. Do not suddenly accelerate or decelerate, as this may cause loss of control or collision.
- Stay alert during operation. Keep NAVI and its activity area within your line of sight to avoid collisions with people or objects.
- Operate NAVI at an appropriate speed. Always maintain a safe distance from other moving people or objects. Slow down when entering unfamiliar environments.
- To avoid distraction, do not wear headphones, earbuds, take calls, take photos, or engage in activities unrelated to operating NAVI while doing so, in order to properly observe the surroundings.
- Exercise caution when operating NAVI in low-visibility environments to avoid personal injury or property damage.
- Do not operate NAVI on slippery surfaces such as ice or glass.
- Do not operate NAVI on elevated surfaces such as tables or beds, or in confined spaces, to avoid falls and injuries.
- Slow down when making turns to avoid NAVI losing control.
- Do not operate NAVI when any component is damaged.
- Do not interfere with NAVI's normal operation to prevent personal injury.
- Do not ride on NAVI, as this may cause personal injury or property damage.
- Do not place heavy loads on NAVI, as this may cause personal injury or property damage.
- Do not modify or disassemble NAVI without authorization.
- Do not replace or install parts using non-original accessories.
- Do not install third-party components on your own, as this may cause compatibility and reliability issues.
- Prevent NAVI from being submerged in liquid, ingesting foreign objects, contacting chemical substances, or being scratched, as these will cause damage.
- Do not clean NAVI using high-pressure water jets or hoses, as water may enter and damage the battery, circuit chips, and other components.
- If NAVI has been caught in rain or water has entered the device, immediately stop use, do not power on, and contact after-sales service promptly.
- Do not perform any operations beyond wiping NAVI dry, as improper handling may cause further damage or void the warranty.
- Avoid leaving NAVI unpowered for extended periods, as this may degrade battery performance.
- Do not repair components, modify circuits or battery packs, or misuse the charger on your own.
- Do not operate NAVI in areas with complex electromagnetic environments or strong interference sources such as mines, transmission towers, high-voltage lines, substations, or nuclear radiation zones, to avoid interference with NAVI's wireless control.
- This product does not support the use of third-party applications.
- Do not use any unofficial system channels or updates, as this may introduce security risks.
- If you violate the above instructions when using NAVI, it may cause damage to NAVI itself or to your or others' personal safety and property. It may also result in the loss of your right to free after-sales service during the warranty period. For specific after-sales policies, please refer to the After-Sales Service Policy.
