# Pre-Operation Guidelines

## Operator Requirements

NAVI is intended for use by individuals aged 14 and above with appropriate cognitive and behavioral capabilities.

Children under 14, elderly individuals, pregnant women, people with disabilities, and others with health conditions that prevent safe operation should only operate NAVI under the guidance and supervision of an adult guardian or trusted companion.

## Pre-Operation Preparation

Before each operation, check NAVI's basic status, including battery level, operating condition, and external appearance. If any abnormality is found, stop use and contact official after-sales support.

If NAVI makes unusual sounds, immediately stop use and contact official after-sales support for professional guidance or repair and inspection services.

If all charging indicator lights are off after powering on, the battery is low and NAVI may not have sufficient power for normal operation. Please ensure that the battery is fully charged before operating.

Before lending NAVI to another person, ensure they have read this manual. You are responsible for the safety of new operators; please help them learn the basic operations. Only allow them to operate once they are familiar with the key operating points.

Do not allow two or more people to control NAVI simultaneously.

When powered on, do not push, pull, or move NAVI's limbs, as this may cause hand injuries.

Please carefully read and follow all "Note," "Warning," "Do Not," and "Avoid" instructions in this manual.
