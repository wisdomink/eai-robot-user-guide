# Fingertip Remote Controller Instructions

The fingertip remote controller is an innovative single-hand line-of-sight remote control product designed to provide an exceptional robot control experience. NAVI is equipped with advanced robotic joints for superior and responsive movement. Using this single-hand controller, you can control NAVI with flexibility and speed, delivering a uniquely enjoyable remote-control experience.

## Initial Pairing Method

**STEP 1:**
Keep NAVI and the fingertip remote controller within 1 m of each other.

**STEP 2:**
When the controller battery has power and the controller is powered off, press and hold the right-side power button for 5 seconds.

![Initial Pairing – Step 2](/images/navi/navi-pairing-step2.png)

**STEP 3:**
When the blue LED changes from fast flashing to steady on, the connection is successful.

![Initial Pairing – Step 3](/images/navi/navi-pairing-step3.png)

## Daily Use

After the initial pairing, the fingertip remote controller will automatically remember NAVI's device information. Subsequently, each time you turn on the remote controller, as long as you are within 5 m of NAVI, the controller will automatically connect without requiring pairing again. You can directly use the controller to control NAVI's movements.

## Fingertip Remote Controller Specifications

> **Note:** The effective control range of the fingertip remote controller is within 5 m; in open areas, range can reach 10 m. Nearby moving people or metal obstacles will significantly affect signal transmission and may cause disconnection. Once the distance meets the minimum communication range, the controller will automatically reconnect to NAVI.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Effective Control Range | ≤5 m (up to 10 m in open areas) |
| Connection | Bluetooth auto-reconnect |
