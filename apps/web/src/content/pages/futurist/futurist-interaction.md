# Voice & Robot Interaction

> **Language-count clarification:** The source says “16 languages” but enumerates 17. The list below is retained from the source; confirm language availability for your software version.

1. The robot provides two voice-interaction entry points, [Built-in Microphone] and [External Microphone], which can be switched in the Voice settings in the client.

2. Voice control of motions, dances, walking, and volume is supported, for example "Please shake hands", "Give me a dance", "Walk forward", "Set the volume to half", and "Set the volume to 30".

3. A newly added mute / interaction-disabled expression layer design (top-left corner of the screen), plus two new immersive expressions: entering the out-of-body avatar and the out-of-body avatar's ultimate move.

4. Multilingual interaction is supported, covering 16 languages including Chinese, English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Thai, Russian, Malay, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Kazakh, and Turkish. Except for Chinese and English, the recognition rate of the other languages is approximately 65%–85%.

5. Speaker recognition is supported. Based on sound-source localization and visual information, the robot achieves "look at whoever is speaking" — turning its head / waist / whole body toward the user — and "knows who is speaking": for users with face ID, FF All-New Futurist can politely address them by name during wake-up responses and LLM chats. In complex environments with medium-to-high noise and multiple people, occasional positioning errors may occur; the [Interaction Full-Body Follow] switch under Client → [Voice] can be turned off.

6. More features will be added in future OTA updates. Please follow the release notes.
