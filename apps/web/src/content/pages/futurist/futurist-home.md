# FF All-New Futurist User Manual

*Based on the supplied V3.2 manual*

FF All-New Futurist is a humanoid robot with 31 active degrees of freedom (excluding hands), RGB binocular vision, voice interaction and a dual-battery power system.

## Before You Start

- Read [Safety Precautions & Guidelines](/futurist/safety) and [Usage Recommendations](/futurist/usage-recommendations).
- Complete the [App Connection Guide](/futurist/quick-start-guide).
- Choose the startup procedure for the [packing box](/futurist/startup-packing), [supine position](/futurist/startup-supine) or [prone position](/futurist/startup-prone).
- Familiarize yourself with [Emergency Stop](/futurist/emergency-stop). An emergency stop removes joint support and can cause the robot to collapse.
