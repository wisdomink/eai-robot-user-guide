# Charging & Battery Replacement

> **Charging clarification:** This section of the source describes charging a removed battery, while its daily-maintenance section says not to disassemble the battery for separate charging and restricts replacement to qualified professionals. Confirm the approved procedure with support before charging a removed pack. Do not open the battery casing.

> **Dual-battery precautions:** Do not remove both packs at once. After inserting the first replacement, wait for its voice prompt to finish before removing the second. Do not charge two under-voltage packs on the robot at the same time.

## Charging the robot with battery installed
