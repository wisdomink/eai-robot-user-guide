# Usage Recommendations

<div class="overflow-x-auto" role="region" aria-label="Scrollable table" tabindex="0">

| No. | Type | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Voice interaction | • Use an external microphone for interaction. |
| 2 | Anthropomorphic walking | • At 0.8 m/s, the robot may trip over its own feet or cross its legs when turning or strafing.<br>• When going up or down slopes, use a speed of 0.6 m/s. The maximum supported slope is 5° (no slope holding). |
| 3 | Full-body dance | • Do not use on soft carpet surfaces thicker than 0.8 cm. |
| 4 | Packing in/out and standing up | • Use only on level ground. Do not use on slippery surfaces (e.g., polished marble). |
| 5 | Battery insertion / removal | • Do not remove both battery packs at the same time. If both packs need to be replaced, wait for the voice prompt to finish after inserting battery pack 1 before removing battery pack 2.<br>• Do not charge two under-voltage battery packs on the robot at the same time. |

</div>
