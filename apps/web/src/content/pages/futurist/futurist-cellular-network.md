# 4G/5G & SIM Cards

Robots are equipped with built-in eSIM cards. For details on the specific installation and warranty services, please refer to [Warranty & Support](/futurist/warranty).

### eSIM Usage Instructions

- Communication standard: Supports 5G/4G.

- Plan: 100 GB of data per month. Once the data is used up, mobile network service stops; to continue using it, contact FF EAI Robotics after-sales to purchase a data add-on package.

- Free period: Up to 1 year. To continue using it after expiry, contact FF EAI Robotics after-sales to renew.

- Activation: Automatically activated after the device powers on.

- Network usage: The eSIM is enabled by default. When the device connects to Wi-Fi, the Wi-Fi network is used preferentially; when Wi-Fi disconnects, the mobile network is used automatically.

### SIM Card Specifications

The FF All-New Futurist supports Micro-SIM cards for internet access. Its cellular communication module is compatible with 4G/5G protocols. Actual network availability may vary by service provider. Please refer to your sales contact for inquiries about SIM Card support.

### SIM Card Purchase

Since some functions of the FF All-New Futurist need to access FF EAI Robotics servers, whitelist configuration is required. You need to purchase dedicated enterprise IoT SIM cards from FF EAI Robotics' partners.

The advantages of enterprise IoT SIM cards: no personal real-name authentication is required; only the business license and the front/back of the legal representative's ID card are needed. In addition, a unified SIM management platform is provided, which allows real-time monitoring of order / dispatch status, traffic usage, and balance / recharge information.

### SIM Card Installation

On the neck of the device, the SIM card slot can be seen by removing the black silicone protective cover.
