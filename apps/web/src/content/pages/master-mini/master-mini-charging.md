# Battery Charging

## 5Ah Battery Charging

Insert the charging plug according to the indicated direction.

![FF Master Mini 5ah connector](/images/master-mini/master-mini-5ah-connector.png)

The charging status is shown as follows: the green light indicates a full charge, and the red light indicates charging in progress.

![FF Master Mini 5ah charge indicators](/images/master-mini/master-mini-5ah-charge-indicators.png)
