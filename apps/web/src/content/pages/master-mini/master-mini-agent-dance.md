# Dance Agent

Specialized in a wide range of Chinese and Western dances, including folk, trendy, and classic dance moves, while also mastering advanced actions such as roundhouse kicks.

💡 Full-body dances require a dedicated venue. Rubber, cement, or tile floors are recommended; high-resistance surfaces such as carpet increase the risk of falling.
