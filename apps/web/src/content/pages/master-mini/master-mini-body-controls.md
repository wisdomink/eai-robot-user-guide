# Power & Body Controls

There are four buttons on the FF Master Mini back panel:

## Power Button

Press and hold for 3 seconds to power on; press and hold again for 3 seconds to power off.

After powering off, wait 6 seconds before powering on again.

WALK: Tap to enter WALK mode.

💡 Make sure the robot has switched to PREP mode and is standing steadily on the ground before switching to WALK mode.

STAND： Tap to enter PREP mode; the robot will fully straighten.

F1： customizable button
