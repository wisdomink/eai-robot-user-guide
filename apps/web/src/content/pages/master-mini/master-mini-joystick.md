# Master Mini Joystick

💡 Note that joystick button mappings vary by software version. Please confirm the current robot software version in the "Check Version" section.

## Joystick Operation

### Power on & off

Power On: Press and hold the Home button for 5 seconds to power on. LED1, LED2, LED3 and LED4 all light up instantly upon startup. LED1 stays on steadily after boot completion.

Power Off: Press and hold the Home button for 5 seconds to power off. LED1, LED2, LED3 and LED4 flash synchronously during shutdown. All indicator lights turn off after shutdown.

### Charging

- Low battery: LED2 flashes as an alert.

- Charging in progress: LED2 keeps flashing.

- Fully charged: LED2 changes to steady on.
