## TECHNICAL DATA


### IDENTIFICATION NUMBERS


#### Vehicle Identification Number

The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is visible in the following location:

![](images/ffom0134.png)

- The top of the dashboard, stamped on a plate that can be seen by looking through the windshield.

> **NOTE:** In the illustration, ‘000...’ is representative of your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).

The Vehicle Identification Number contains the following information:

![](images/ffom0135.png)

1. World Manufacturing Identifier: 7G0 = Faraday Future.
2. Trim level: 90A = Futurist Alliance, 90F = Futurist, 90S = Standard.
3. Motor/Battery/Drivetrain: M = 3 Motor/Extended Range.
4. Restraint information. R = Manual seat belts, driver front & knee airbags, passenger front & knee airbags, side inflatable airbags for rows 1 and 2.
5. Check digit: Mathematically formulated.
6. Model Year: P = 2023, R = 2024, S = 2025, T = 2026.
7. Factory Code: H = Hanford, California.
8. Unique serial number, six digits.

> **NOTE:** The VIN is also printed on the certification label, located on the driver’s side door pillar and visible when the driver’s door is open. See [Vehicle Certification Label].
