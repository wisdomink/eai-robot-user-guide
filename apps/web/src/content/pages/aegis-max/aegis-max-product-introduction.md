# Product Overview & Components

FX Aegis Max is a wheel-legged quadruped robot featuring lightweight design, high load capacity, long endurance, strong protection, and flexible, stable movement. Each leg is equipped with three joint motors and one hub motor, forming a wheeled leg. It is equipped with sensors including LiDAR, optical cameras, ultrasonic radar, IMU, fill lights and RTK modules. Internally it incorporates a high-performance main platform for motion control, autonomous navigation and positioning, and environmental detection, and it provides a rich set of power supply and communication interfaces supporting the expansion of multiple categories of mission payload.
