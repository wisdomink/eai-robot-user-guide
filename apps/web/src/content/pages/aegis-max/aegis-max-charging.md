# Charging

## Remove the Battery

1. Place the robot in a horizontal prone posture: abdomen fully against the ground, legs on either side, lower legs retracted, and all four knee joints and wheel-legs flat on the ground. Lift the front and rear legs on the battery side in sequence to make room for removal.

   Alternatively, before powering off, select the battery swapping posture. The robot assumes a horizontal prone posture and lifts the front and rear legs on the battery side automatically.

2. Gently pull the battery hand strap to unlock the latch, then pull out the battery.

   ![Battery removal posture](/images/aegis-max/aegis-mega-a-d1-14.png)

## Charging Preparation

Use a dry, cool location away from flammable or explosive materials. The ambient temperature around the charger cradle must be 0°C–50°C.

1. Inspect the battery exterior for bulging, leakage or damage.
2. Place the charger cradle on a hard, flat surface so it is stable and cannot tip over.
3. Connect the charger to AC mains and turn on the cradle's rocker switch.

   A steady red light means the cradle is connected and powered on.

   ![Charger cradle and battery insertion](/images/aegis-max/aegis-mega-a-d1-15.png)

## Battery Charging

1. Align the battery with the cradle's charging interface and insert it into the holder. Single-battery and dual-battery charging are supported.

   A steady green cradle indicator corresponding to the battery indicates stable charging.

   The battery LEDs illuminate in sequence. A steady LED indicates a fully charged segment; a rapidly flashing LED indicates the segment being charged.

2. When all four battery indicators stay on, promptly disconnect the battery or the cradle power source. Charging must not exceed 6 hours.
3. After removing a fully charged battery, promptly turn off the cradle to avoid leaving its plug live and creating an electric-shock hazard.

**Warning:** Do not use third-party chargers or charger cradles.

![Original battery charger](/images/aegis-max/aegis-mega-a-d1-16.png)

## Remote Control Charging

- Charge the remote control with the original USB charger (AC 100–240 V, USB Type-C).

- During charging, the battery indicator illuminates in sequence to display progress. A steady LED denotes a fully charged segment; a rapidly flashing LED denotes the segment currently charging.

- To maintain the remote control battery in optimal condition, fully charge it once a month.
