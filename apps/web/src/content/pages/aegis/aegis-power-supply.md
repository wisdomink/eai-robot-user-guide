## Charging Instructions

Please ensure that both operators and non-operators present have carefully read this manual and are familiar with the basic operating procedures and safety precautions.

1. **Connect the charging dock** Insert the charging cable plug into the bottom port of the charging dock.

![](/images/aegis/aegis-power-supply-6.png)

2. **Connect the power supply** After confirming the connection is secure, insert the power plug into the socket.

![](/images/aegis/aegis-power-supply-7.png)

3. **Place the battery** Insert the battery into the charging dock; charging begins.

![](/images/aegis/aegis-power-supply-8.png)

**To ensure electrical safety:**

1. Please follow the steps in the above order and avoid connecting the power supply before the charging dock.
2. Do not plug or unplug the charging dock port while it is powered.
