## Disposal

This product contains a rechargeable lithium battery pack. It is harmful to the environment when discarded. Remove the battery before disposal and hand it to a professional recycling agency.

- Before removing the battery, power off the device and deplete the battery charge as much as possible.
- Do not damage the battery casing.
- The removed battery must be handed over to a professional recycling agency for proper disposal.
