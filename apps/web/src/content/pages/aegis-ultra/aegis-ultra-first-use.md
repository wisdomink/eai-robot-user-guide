# Preparation Before First Use

## Start Up

1. Remove the robot dog from the packaging box and place it on a flat surface.

2. Take out the battery and insert it into the battery compartment.

3. Press the battery power button briefly once, then press and hold for 5 seconds to power on.

4. Wait for the boot sequence to complete (blue LED steady = boot complete).

## Shut Down

1. Ensure the robot dog is in a lying-down position.

2. Press and hold the power button for over 3 seconds.

3. Once all LEDs on the battery have turned off, the device is in shutdown state.

## Emergency Stop

1. Press L1 + R1 simultaneously on the remote controller, OR use the Emergency Stop option in the APP.

   The robot will activate soft-stop mode and automatically lie down.

2. Resume normal operation only when it is safe to do so.
