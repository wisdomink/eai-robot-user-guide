# Remote Control User Guide

## Connect the Remote Controller

![Remote controller buttons](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-07.jpeg)

1. Press and hold the power button at the top of the remote controller to power on.

2. Tap "Smart Robot Controller" to launch the application.

   ![Launch Smart Robot Controller](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-08.jpeg)

   The app main page will be displayed.

   ![App main page](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-09.jpeg)

3. Tap the button in the upper left corner to enter System General Settings.

   ![Open general settings](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-10.jpeg)

   ![General settings](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-11.jpeg)

4. Tap "Not Connected" in the upper right corner to start the remote controller connection.

   ![Connect the controller](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-12.jpeg)

5. Select the Wi-Fi network of the robot dog to connect. The Wi-Fi name is found on the device nameplate (format: "XGO13BW001").

   ![Select robot Wi-Fi](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-13.jpeg)

   The app confirms successful pairing.

   ![Connection successful](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-14.jpeg)

## Operating Modes

### Normal Mode and Laboratory Mode

Normal Mode: Stable movement, standard actions — daily use.

Laboratory Mode: Enhanced performance and additional special actions for research and demonstration scenarios.

<div class="overflow-x-auto" role="region" aria-label="Scrollable table" tabindex="0">

| Category | Action | Normal Mode | Lab Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal/laboratory mode switch |  | App | App |
| General Actions | Stand | APP | APP |
| General Actions | Stand Damping (Slowly Lie Down) | APP | APP |
| General Actions | Lie Down / Emergency Stop | L1+R1 or APP | L1+R1 or APP |
| Movement actions | Forward / Backward / Left / Right | Forward / Backward / Left / Right | Forward / Backward / Left / Right |
| In-place Actions | Idle/Moving Switch | APP | APP |
| In-place Actions | Pitch Adjustment | Push Right Joystick FWD/Back | Push Right Joystick FWD/Back |
| In-place Actions | Horizontal Turn | Push Right Joystick L/R | Push Right Joystick L/R |
| In-place Actions | Left/Right Head Tilt | Y/A | Y/A |
| In-place Actions | High / Low Posture | X / B | X / B |
| Special Actions | Jump Up | — | APP |
| Special Actions | Forward Jump | — | APP |
| Special Actions | Backflip (Ultra only — not available on Ultra-W) | — | APP |
| Special Actions | Waving Greeting | — | APP |
| Special Actions | Biped Standing | — | APP |

</div>

> **WARNING:** Special actions in Rage Mode may affect the device lifespan.

Performing special actions in Laboratory Mode imposes heavy loads on the joint modules. Prolonged operation may cause accelerated joint wear and significantly reduce service life.

Please enable with caution and reasonably control the duration of use to ensure long-term stability of the device.

### Enable Turbo Mode

1. Tap the settings button on the upper left.

   ![Open Turbo Mode settings](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-15.jpeg)

2. Enable Turbo Mode.

   ![Enable Turbo Mode](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-16.jpeg)

3. A Turbo Mode activation prompt will appear. If confirmed, tap “Confirm” to enable it.

   ![Confirm Turbo Mode](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-17.jpeg)

   Turbo Mode is now enabled.

   ![Turbo Mode enabled](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-18.jpeg)

## Basic Control

1. Tap the control button in the lower right corner to enter control page.

   ![Open basic control](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-19.jpeg)

2. You can control the robot dog’s movement and posture changes by tapping the center button on the screen, using the virtual joystick, or virtual buttons.

   ![Movement and posture controls](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-20.jpeg)

## Recording and Screenshots

### Screen Recording

1. Tap the screen recording button in the lower right corner of the screen to start recording.

   ![Start screen recording](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-21.jpeg)

2. Recording in progress. Tap the red stop button to end recording.

   ![Stop recording](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-22.jpeg)

   Recorded videos can be viewed in the remote controller’s gallery.

   ![Open recorded videos](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-23.jpeg)

### Screenshots

1. Tap the screenshot button in the lower right corner to take a screenshot.

   ![Take a screenshot](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-24.jpeg)

   Screenshots can be viewed in the remote controller’s gallery.

   ![Open screenshots](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-25.jpeg)

## Target Tracking

1. Tap the settings button in the upper left corner.

   ![Open tracking settings](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-26.jpeg)

2. Select the tracking function.

   ![Enable tracking](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-27.jpeg)

   Red boxes identify all trackable targets.

3. Tap to select a target, then tap “Start Tracking” to activate tracking.

   ![Select a tracking target](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-28.jpeg)

   ![Tracking active](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-29.jpeg)

   Tracking continues once activated. Cancel tracking in time to avoid accidentally targeting unintended objects.

## Video Stream

1. Tap the settings button in the upper left corner.

   ![Open video settings](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-30.jpeg)

2. Select the Video Stream toggle and save the settings.

   ![Video stream toggle](/images/aegis-ultra/aegis-ultra-0921-31.jpeg)
