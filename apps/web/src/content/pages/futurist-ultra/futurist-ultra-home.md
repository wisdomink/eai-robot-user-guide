# FF All-New Futurist Ultra User Manual

*Based on the supplied FF All-New Futurist Ultra V3.2 manual*

FF All-New Futurist Ultra is a humanoid robot with 51–71 active degrees of freedom, 3D LiDAR, RGB-D cameras and a dual-battery power system. The manual lists a height of 1.74 m, a weight of 60 kg and a battery capacity of 1152 W·h.

## Before You Start

- Read [Safety Precautions & Guidelines](/futurist-ultra/safety-protection-measures).
- Complete the [App Connection Guide](/futurist-ultra/quick-start-guide).
- Choose startup from the [packing box](/futurist-ultra/startup-packing), [supine position](/futurist-ultra/startup-supine) or [prone position](/futurist-ultra/startup-prone).
- Learn [Emergency Stop](/futurist-ultra/emergency-stop) before operation; releasing joint support can cause the robot to collapse.
