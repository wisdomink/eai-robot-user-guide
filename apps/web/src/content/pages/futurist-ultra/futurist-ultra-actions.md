# Dance & Body Actions

## Full-Body Dance

In the Talent screen of the skill operation navigation bar, select the corresponding dance content and swipe to play.

### Step 1: Tap the top tab bar and select [Skills]

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Dance & Body Actions](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-67.png)

### Step 2: Select the desired dance, and swipe to execute

> ⚠️ Note: Different dances must be confirmed and executed according to their respective pop-up instructions. Follow the prompts to ensure safety.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Dance & Body Actions](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-68.png)

## Body Actions

### Upper-Limb Actions

On the client home page, tap Skills &gt; Resource Management, then select an action to play.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Dance & Body Actions](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-69.png)

### Upper-Limb Compliant Motion Playback (Passive Safety)

When this switch is turned on, the robot's arms become compliant, which reduces collision force.

### Upper-Limb Motion Perception-Based Obstacle Stop (Active Safety)

When this switch is turned on, if you stand within the range of the robot's motion, the arm will pause for your safety (staying within 20 cm of the robot may affect the obstacle-stop performance).

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Dance & Body Actions](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-70.png)
