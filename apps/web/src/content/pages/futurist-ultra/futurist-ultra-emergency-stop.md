# Emergency Stop

The emergency stop function is used for the emergency stop of the robot in case of sudden situations, preventing further damage to the robot or the surrounding environment, and can be triggered in the "Soft Emergency Stop" mode.

After the robot enters the emergency stop state, its limbs will lose power and fall to the ground, with a certain risk of damaging the machine. Please ensure that users use the emergency stop function with extreme caution.

## Triggering the Soft Emergency Stop

1. Steps to trigger the emergency stop state

Method 1: Triggered via the App

Operation Steps

Operation Interface

#### Press the floating ball to enter the emergency stop page

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra operation step](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-58.png)

#### Press and hold the [Emergency Stop] button, then slide it to the right until it reaches the end to trigger a soft emergency stop

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra operation step](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-59.png)

#### Enter the soft emergency stop state

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra operation step](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-60.png)

Method 2: Triggered by remote control buttons (applicable to remote controls)

When the remote control is connected to the robot, hold the four buttons L1+L2+R1+R2 at the same time for more than 0.5 seconds to trigger the soft emergency stop state.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Emergency Stop](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-61.png)

2. Operable items in the soft emergency stop state

After the robot enters the soft emergency stop state, only limited operations such as viewing the function list and alarm information, and reporting problems, can be performed on the floating ball page.

3. Steps to exit the soft emergency stop state

Restart the robot to exit the soft emergency stop state.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Emergency Stop](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-62.png)

## Emergency Stop Settings

Turn on/off the soft emergency stop function: On the [Settings] - [Movement] page of the App, you can turn the software emergency stop function on and off.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Emergency Stop](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-63.png)
