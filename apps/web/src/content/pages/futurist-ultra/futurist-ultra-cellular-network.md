# 4G/5G & SIM Cards

## 4G/5G Module User Guide

Robots are equipped with built-in eSIM cards. For details on the specific configuration and warranty services, please refer to Section 8.1 of this document.

### eSIM Usage Instructions

- Communication standard: Supports 5G/4G.

- Plan: 100 GB of data per month. Once the data is used up, mobile network service stops; to continue using it, contact FF EAI Robotics after-sales to purchase a data add-on package.

- Free period: Up to 1 year. To continue using it after expiry, contact FF EAI Robotics after-sales to renew.

- Activation: Automatically activated after the device powers on.

- Network usage: The eSIM is enabled by default. When the device connects to Wi-Fi, the Wi-Fi network is used preferentially; when Wi-Fi disconnects, the mobile network is used automatically.

### SIM Card Specifications

The All-New Futurist Ultra supports Micro-SIM cards for internet access. Its cellular communication module is compatible with 4G/5G protocols, and supports carriers including China Mobile, China Unicom, and China Telecom.

### SIM Card Purchase

Because some functions of the All-New Futurist Ultra require access to FF EAI Robotics' servers, whitelist configuration is required, and a dedicated enterprise IoT card must be purchased from an FF EAI Robotics partner. The advantage of an enterprise IoT card is that no personal real-name verification is required—only the business license and the front and back of the legal representative's ID are needed. In addition, it supports a unified card management platform that can monitor in real time the card's order and delivery status, data usage, balance and renewal, etc.

### SIM Card Insertion

Flip the head upward and insert the SIM card into the corresponding slot from the neck of the robot.
