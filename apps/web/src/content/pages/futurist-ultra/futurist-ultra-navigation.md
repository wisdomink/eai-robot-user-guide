# Navigation & Lively Standby

The switch is on by default. Once you turn it off, the robot will no longer avoid obstacles, posing a collision risk — please use this option with caution.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Navigation & Lively Standby](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-71.png)

## Lively Standby

The [Lively Standby] switch is off by default. It can be enabled under [Settings] &gt; [Upper-Body Actions] &gt; [Lively Standby] in the App.
