# Startup from the Packing Box

## Packaging Box Startup

### Step 1: Activate the battery

Note: This activation step is only required when opening the package for the first time or when the battery pack is in storage and transportation mode.

Stand the packaging box upright, open the bottom window, then short press and long press (1s) the power button of each of the two battery packs, until all the indicator lights of the battery packs light up in sequence, indicating successful activation. Then close the bottom window.

#### Step 1-1: Stand up the packing box and open the bottom window

![Step 1-1: Stand up the packing box and open the bottom window](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-21.png)

#### Step 1-2: Activate two battery packs

![Step 1-2: Activate two battery packs](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-22.png)

### Step 2: Open the package

Lay the packing box flat, lock the wheels at the bottom of the packing box after confirming that there are no obstacles in front, then open the top cover of the packing box and remove the middle layer and hip-cover foam, ensuring that there are no items above the robot.

#### Step 2-1: Place the packing box flat

![Step 2-1: Place the packing box flat](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-23.png)

#### Step 2-2: Lock the wheels at the bottom of the packing box

![Step 2-2: Lock the wheels at the bottom of the packing box](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-24.png)

#### Step 2-3: Unlock and open the top cover of the packaging box

![Step 2-3: Unlock and open the top cover of the packaging box](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-25.png)

#### Step 2-4: Remove the middle layer and hip-cover foam

![Step 2-4: Remove the middle layer and hip-cover foam](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-26.png)

### Step 3: Power on and start

Press and hold the robot's power button to turn on the robot. After the robot is powered on, the head screen and the indicator light on the chest will light up.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Startup from the Packing Box](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-27.png)

### Step 4: Connect to the App

Complete the connection between the App and the robot according to the steps in Section 4.1, and select the [Packaging Box Startup] card on the [Please Select Robot Posture] page.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Startup from the Packing Box](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-28.png)

### Step 5: Stand up inside the box

After confirming the environment step by step in the App, control the robot to stand up from the box with one click. The robot will then enter the motion state and maintain a stable standing posture.

> ⚠️ Note: Observe the following:
> • Ensure no obstacles are present in front of the packaging box.
> • Lock all casters of the box securely.
> • Make sure the robot's hip position is above the red line; adjust it manually above the red line if not.
> • Do not touch the robot during the stand-up process.
> • After the robot stands up, move the packaging box at least 0.5 meters away before operation.
