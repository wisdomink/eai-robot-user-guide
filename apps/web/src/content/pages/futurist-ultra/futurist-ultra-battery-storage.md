# Battery Storage Mode

> ⚠️ Note: Before transportation or long-term storage, it is recommended to enable the battery pack storage mode. If the robot is powered off without entering Storage Mode, the system will still consume approximately 7W of standby power, and a fully charged battery pack may be completely depleted in about 7 days.

## Enter Storage Mode

### Step 1: Press the battery pack power button briefly 5 times continuously. After the red indicator starts flashing, press and hold the power button.

### Step 2: Continue holding the button until the green indicator starts flashing, then release the button. The battery pack has now entered Storage Mode.

Storage Mode Activation Check:

Briefly press the battery pack power button once. If the battery indicator lights up only after a few seconds, Storage Mode has been successfully activated.

## Exit Storage Mode

### Step 1: Briefly press the battery pack power button once to make all green indicators light up, and perform the Storage Mode check.

### Step 2: Immediately press and hold the power button again. When the indicator light sequence completes, the battery pack has been successfully activated and has exited Storage Mode.
