# 2. Product Overview

## 2.1 Product Introduction

FF Futurist is an intelligent general-purpose robot designed for a variety of tasks. It has abilities such as navigation, operation, identification, and interaction.

The robot is equipped with multiple sensors and employs LiDAR-based simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), which allows the robot to operate in polymorphic indoor environments.

The model naming rules for this robot can be found in the `Settings` section of FF Robotics App.

This machine complies with part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions:

1. This device may not cause harmful interference.
2. This device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.
