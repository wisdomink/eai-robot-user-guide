# Product Overview

The FF All-New Futurist series products feature 31 to 71 degrees of freedom (varies by model and configuration), enabling flexible movement and high-precision posture control. The following details the specific configuration and feature highlights of the FF All-New Futurist Ultra.

- FF All-New Futurist Ultra: 2 degrees of freedom in the neck, 7 per arm, 3 in the waist, and 6 per leg.

- The perception system is equipped with front-view RGB stereo cameras, surround-view triple fisheye cameras, LiDAR, GPS RTK, UWB positioning, shoulder touch sensors, a chest RGB-D camera, and a hip RGB-D camera.

- The communication system is equipped with Wi-Fi, 4G/5G mobile network, and Bluetooth.

- The interaction system is equipped with omnidirectional microphones, speakers, a curved display, and omnidirectional indicator lights.

- The power system is equipped with a 1152Wh battery, providing a total battery life of 8 hours, including 4 hours of standing operation and over 4 hours of continuous walking. It supports direct charging, battery changing, with a charging time of ≤2 hours.
