# Charging & Battery Replacement

> **Source clarification:** This chapter describes charging a removed battery, while the daily-maintenance chapter restricts battery replacement to qualified professionals and says not to disassemble the battery for separate charging. Confirm the applicable procedure with support.

## Charging Mode

1. Charging the robot with battery installed

When both batteries are in place, the magnetic charging head of the charger can be connected to the charging port of the robot. After the power is turned on, the magnetic charging head of the charger lights up green, indicating successful charging. The schematic diagram is as follows:

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Charging & Battery Replacement](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-50.png)

2. Charging the battery solely

Remove the battery and separate the magnetic adapter section of the charger to directly charge the battery. The schematic diagram is as follows:

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Charging & Battery Replacement](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-51.png)
