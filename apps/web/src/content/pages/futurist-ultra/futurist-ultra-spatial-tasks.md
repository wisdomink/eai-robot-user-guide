# Maps & Spatial Tasks

## Mapping and Point Marking

1. Open the client and enter the Map module.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-72.png)

2. Tap Create Map and complete mapping of the exhibition area.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-73.png)

3. Enter Edit → Points and mark each explanation point on the map.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-72.png)

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-74.png)

4. After editing, upload the map to the cloud.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-75.png)

## Post-Deployment Verification

Task deployment confirmation. Open the client, enter the Task module, and confirm that the latest deployed spatial task is visible.

After receiving the task, trigger the spatial task in the following three ways to verify that the function works properly:

<div class="overflow-x-auto" role="region" aria-label="Scrollable table" tabindex="0">

| Trigger Method | Verification |
| --- | --- |
| Client trigger | Trigger the spatial task manually in the client app and confirm the task starts normally |
| Voice trigger | Trigger the spatial task with a voice command and confirm voice recognition and task startup work normally |
| Shoulder-tap trigger | Trigger the spatial task by tapping the robot's shoulder and confirm motion recognition and task startup work normally |

</div>

Pass criteria: the robot can execute the exhibition explanation task normally along the preset route and points.

## Client Trigger Method

Tap the filter to show the required map. Deployed tasks will appear here. Then, tap [Start] under a task, and the robot will begin executing that task.

![FF All-New Futurist Ultra — Maps & Spatial Tasks](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-ultra-v32-76.png)
