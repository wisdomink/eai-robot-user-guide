# Charging

FX Aegis Mega D uses dual batteries and supports replacing either battery while the robot remains powered on. Batteries can be charged in the charging hub.

## Charging Conditions

- Recommended ambient temperature: 0°C–40°C.
- Monitor the battery and charging hub throughout charging.
- Disconnect charging power promptly after charging is complete.

## Remove the Battery

1. Make the robot lie down. Lift the front or rear legs enough to allow removal of the corresponding battery.
2. Hold the battery strap and pull it outward with appropriate force to release the buckle, then remove the battery from its compartment.

   ![Battery removal](/images/aegis-mega-d/aegis-mega-d-14.png)

## Connect the Charging Hub

1. Insert the battery with its power button facing inward.
2. Connect the charging hub to AC mains (100–240 V).
3. Turn on the charging hub switch.

   ![Charging hub connection](/images/aegis-mega-d/aegis-mega-d-15.png)

## Charging Progress and Completion

During charging, the battery LEDs light in sequence. The number illuminated indicates the amount charged.

When charging is complete, all four battery LEDs turn off and the charger indicator turns green. Disconnect the charging power promptly.
