# Product Overview & Components

## Overview

FX Aegis Mega D is a wheeled-legged quadruped robot with 4 motors on each leg, a total of 16 degrees of freedom (DOF), and a variety of movement methods. The robot is equipped with 2 wide-angle cameras, 2 laser radars, and 3 industrial-grade high-performance hosts for motion control, environmental perception calculation, navigation and inspections.
