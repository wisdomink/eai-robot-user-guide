# Startup & Shutdown

## Prepare and Install the Batteries

1. Remove the robot from the transport case and place it on a flat surface. Follow the [Carrying](/aegis-mega-d/preparation#carrying) guidance.
2. Lift the front legs enough to insert the front battery. Orient the battery with its LED and button above the battery strap.
3. Pull the strap outward with one hand to unlock the buckle. With the other hand, lift the bottom of the battery slightly and push it into the compartment.
4. Release the strap. Installation is complete when the buckles on both sides engage in the compartment. Install the rear battery in the same way.
5. Adjust the robot's posture: the upper leg should be close against the lower leg, with the lower leg and wheel resting naturally on the ground.

   ![Startup posture](/images/aegis-mega-d/aegis-mega-d-16.png)

## Power On

Before startup, confirm that the hard emergency stop is released. If it is engaged, troubleshoot first. Once safe operation is confirmed, rotate the button in the direction of its arrow to release it.

1. Press, then press and hold the battery button until the battery LED flashes and stays on.

   The battery is now supplying power, and the LEDs show its current charge level.

2. Press the robot power button on the right side of the body.

   The power switch stays green as the robot starts.

## Connect the Controller

1. Ensure that the controller has enough charge, then press and hold its power button.

   ![Controller power button](/images/aegis-mega-d/aegis-mega-d-17.png)

2. Open the “Faraday Future” app.

   The controller and robot are already paired; the app connects automatically to the bound robot.

See [Controller & Settings](/aegis-mega-d/remote-control) for the interface and button functions.

## Power Off

Make sure the robot is sitting before shutdown.

1. Press the robot power button. It pops up, the power and status indicators go out, and the robot shuts down.
2. Press, then press and hold the battery button until the battery LED flashes once.

   The battery indicator goes out, confirming that it has stopped supplying power and shutdown is complete.
