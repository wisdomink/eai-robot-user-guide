# FX Aegis Mega D User Manual

*Wheeled-leg quadruped robot*

![FX Aegis Mega D wheeled-leg robot](/images/aegis-mega-d/aegis-mega-d-01.png)

FX Aegis Mega D has 16 degrees of freedom, dual wide-angle cameras and dual LiDAR sensors for motion control, environmental perception, navigation and inspection.

- Follow [Preparation & Carrying](/aegis-mega-d/preparation) and [Startup & Shutdown](/aegis-mega-d/startup-shutdown).
- Locate the [Emergency Stop & Protection](/aegis-mega-d/emergency) controls before operation.
- See [Product Specifications](/aegis-mega-d/specifications) for operating limits.
