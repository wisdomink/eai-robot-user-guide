Data above are measured under ideal conditions and dual battery powered mode, the actual results may be biased.

**Operating limits:** The manual lists 5.0 m/s and 50 kg as extreme-test data. Actual product speed is limited to 3.0 m/s, maximum working speed is 2.0 m/s, and recommended installed payload is no more than 15 kg. See [Payload & Expansion Ports](/aegis-mega-d/payload).


**Charger output:** The source lists “84/3A (for single battery)” without a voltage unit. Confirm the output rating on the charger label; no missing unit has been assumed here.
