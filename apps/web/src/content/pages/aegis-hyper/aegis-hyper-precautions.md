# Precautions

![FX Aegis Hyper figure 22](/images/aegis-hyper/aegis-hyper-figure-22.png)

## 3.1 Work Environment

- Please do not operate the robot in environments with strong WiFi

signal interference. Be sure to turn off part or all other WiFi signal

sources, and then use the joystick to operate the robot.

- Do not operate the robot in such bad weather as fog, snowing,

lightning, sandstorms, windstorms, tornadoes, etc.

- Keep the robot in sight and always keep it at least 2 meters away from

people, water, open flames, etc.

- When using the robot on smooth surfaces such as glass and tiles,

avoid violent movements and apply Stair or Slope gait to prevent the

robot from slipping and falling down.

- Do not operate the robot on the edge of a high place to prevent it from

falling and causing damage.

- Please consult the after-sales staff first before operating the robot in

environments with strong electromagnetic interference.

## 3.2 Other Precautions

- When carrying the robot, pay attention to the anti-pinch label on the

robot and do not put your hands into the position where the anti-pinch

label is attached!

- Do not lift the robot while it is moving to avoid unintended movements

that could cause damage to robot or injury to people!

- Please do not download other unrelated APPs in the controller.

- Pay attention to the sealing label on the robot. It is strictly forbidden

to disassemble the robot personally. Once disassembled, the warranty

will be invalid!
