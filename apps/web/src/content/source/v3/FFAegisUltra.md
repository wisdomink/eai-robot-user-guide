# Usage Restrictions & Safety Notes&#x20;

### Usage Restrictions&#x20;

* This product must not be used by individuals with physical, sensory, or mental impairments, or by those lacking relevant experience and knowledge (including children), unless supervised or instructed by a responsible guardian to ensure safe operation.&#x20;

* Do not allow children to treat this product as a toy. Supervise children and pets during operation to keep them at a safe distance.&#x20;

* If the power cord is damaged, it must be replaced with a special cord or component obtained from the manufacturer or authorized service provider.&#x20;

* Do not operate the product in elevated areas without protective barrier&#x20;

* If the product exhibits abnormal behavior such as leg flailing or violent shaking, press the Emergency Stop button to activate the soft-stop safety mode. The robot will automatically lie down. Resume operation only after troubleshooting and resolving the issue.&#x20;

* Do not place the product upside down. Do not use the robot's legs as handles for carrying.&#x20;

* Do not operate the product in environments above 40°C or below 0°C.&#x20;

* Do not operate the product in areas with electromagnetic interference.&#x20;

* Do not operate the product in environments with Wi-Fi or 2.4G signal interference. Before starting the product, ensure there is at least 1 meter of clear space around it, with no people or objects in the motion path, to prevent collisions.&#x20;

* When the product is ascending or descending stairs or slopes, do not stand below it to avoid injury in case of a fall.&#x20;

* Do not place children, pets, or any objects on the robot, whether it is in motion or stationery.&#x20;

* Do not insert hands into the range of motion of the robot's joints during transport, to avoid pinching or cutting.&#x20;

* Keep body parts away from moving components.&#x20;

* Power off the robot and unplug the power cord before cleaning or performing maintenance on the robot or charging dock.&#x20;

* Do not wipe the robot or charge dock with a wet cloth or rinse them with any liquids.&#x20;

* Do not use third-party batteries, power cords, or charging docks. Only the charging dock included should be used.&#x20;

# Preparation & Remote-Control Guide&#x20;

### Preparation Before First Use&#x20;

**Start Up**&#x20;

* Remove the robot dog from the packaging box and place it on a flat surface.&#x20;

* Take out the battery and insert it into the battery compartment.&#x20;

* Press the battery power button briefly once, then press and hold for 5 seconds to power on.&#x20;

* Wait for the boot sequence to complete (blue LED steady = boot complete).&#x20;

**Shut Down**&#x20;

* Ensure the robot dog is in a lying-down position.&#x20;

* Press and hold the power button for over 3 seconds.&#x20;

* Once all LEDs on the battery have turned off, the device is in shutdown state.&#x20;

**Emergency Stop**&#x20;

* Press L1 + R1 simultaneously on the remote controller, OR use the Emergency Stop option in the APP.&#x20;

* The robot will activate soft-stop mode and automatically lie down.&#x20;

* Resume normal operation only when it is safe to do so.&#x20;
