## Power On/Off Instructions

### Pre-Startup Posture Requirements (Illustrated)
When powered off, NAVI cannot autonomously adjust its joint positions. Therefore, before powering on, you must manually position NAVI’s four limbs into the standard startup posture to ensure it can stand normally after powering on. The standard startup posture is shown below.
![NAVI Pre-Startup Posture](/images/navi/navi-startup-posture.png)

Before powering, ensure NAVI is in the standard startup posture shown above.

### Power Button Location
![NAVI Power Button Location](/images/navi/navi-power-button.png)


### How to Power On
Follow these steps:
Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds. Upon hearing a “humming” tone, release the button to complete the startup. The button light will illuminate, and the battery indicator lights will flash in sequence, displaying the current battery level.

### How to Power Off
To ensure NAVI responds flexibly and precisely to shutdown commands, NAVI offers the following two shutdown methods. Please wait until NAVI has completely stopped moving and safely crouched down before shutting off.
*Note: Do not power off while NAVI is moving or standing. Once shutdown is triggered, NAVI will immediately lose power, which may cause personal injury or property damage.

#### Standard Shutdown
Press and hold the power button for 3 to 5 seconds; you will hear a long “humming” tone.
Release the button after the tone; NAVI will automatically shut down once all indicator lights go off.

#### Force Shutdown
*Note: Use this only when standard shutdown cannot be completed.
Press and hold the power button for more than 6 seconds.
Release after all indicator lights go off; NAVI will force shut down.

### Accidental Power Button Activation

#### When No Pre-Startup Preparation Is Needed
When NAVI has crouched down due to a depleted battery, it can autonomously adjust its joint positions, so no limb adjustment is needed before powering on.

### Powering On Outside the Box
If you are starting NAVI outside the box, please observe the following pre-startup conditions.

#### Hard and Level Surface
The surface should not visibly indent under pressure. Avoid starting on uneven surfaces or overly soft carpets.

#### No Obvious Obstructions on the Surface
There should be no visible obstructions or protrusions on the surface contacting NAVI’s legs and belly. Avoid items such as cardboard or children’s toys that may affect NAVI’s static posture.

#### Lower Legs Folded to Structural Stop
Deviation of up to 18° is acceptable. The lower legs should be in close contact with the upper legs.

#### Hip Joint Actuator Angle Requirements
The hip joint and thigh angle deviation should be within 18°. Adjust the thigh and hip joint angle so that both the foot pads and knee joints flush with the ground.

## Network Settings

### Connecting to the Robot Dog Hotspot via App
The App will automatically connect to the robot dog hotspot by default. If autoconnection fails, follow these steps:
- Confirm that the robot dog is activated and powered on.
- If you have multiple robot dogs, go to “Pet” in the top-left corner of the App home screen and select the target robot dog under “Switch Pet.”
- Tap the “Pair” button on the App home screen; the App will connect to the robot dog hotspot.

#### Troubleshooting
If pairing fails after tapping “Pair,” manually connect to the robot dog hotspot as follows:
- On the App home screen, go to General > Wi-Fi & Security > Copy Password.
- In your phone network settings, select the robot dog Wi-Fi, paste the password, and connect to the robot dog hotspot.

### Configuring Wi-Fi for the Robot Dog via App
- Confirm that the App is connected to the robot dog hotspot.
- Tap “Network Connection” on the App home screen to enter the network configuration page.
- Select the target Wi-Fi, enter the password, and tap “Confirm” to complete the setup.

#### Troubleshooting
During setup, scanning the Wi-Fi list may cause your phone to disconnect from the robot dog hotspot, prompting “Network configuration complete, please check connection shortly.” Reconnect to the robot dog hotspot and configure Wi-Fi again.

### Using the Robot Dog’s 4G Network
If you have not paired a pet in the App, the robot dog’s 4G network is used by default. We recommend configuring Wi-Fi for the pet after pairing.
If the connected Wi-Fi signal is weak, you can switch to 4G in the App’s home screen under Network Connection.

## Maintenance and Care

### Usage Care

#### Post-Operation Care
- Stop NAVI on a relatively flat surface before powering off.
- Always use the original charger to prevent battery damage or fire risk.

#### Parts Requiring Regular Maintenance or Replacement

#### Main Unit Maintenance
- Check that all parts of NAVI are in good condition. If in doubt, contact the after-sales service team.
- Before performing maintenance or cleaning, ensure NAVI is powered off and the power adapter is disconnected.
- When replacing accessories, use manufacturer-specified parts to avoid damage caused by incompatibility.
- The foot pads are consumable parts. Severe wear, missing corners, or detachment may cause NAVI to move or stand unstably.
- Regularly check battery level and health. When battery life noticeably decreases, contact customer service for a battery replacement.
- Store NAVI and the power adapter in a cool, dry place.

#### Battery Maintenance
- Never store the battery in an environment where the temperature exceeds 45°C or falls below -10°C. Long-term storage under such conditions will degrade battery performance.
- For long-term storage (more than 3 months), store the battery at 20°C to 25°C with a relative humidity of 0 to 60% RH. The battery charge should not exceed 80%; 30% to 50% is recommended.
- During storage, periodically check the battery level. If it drops below 20%, recharge the battery. It is recommended to recharge at least once every 3 months to maintain battery activity.
- The recommended charging temperature for the battery is 10°C to 25°C.
- Do not charge the battery for more than 24 hours, as overcharging will shorten battery life.
- After under-voltage protection is triggered, the battery cannot output power; it must be charged before normal operation can resume.
- Avoid deep discharge of the battery, as this may permanently disable it and make it unable to charge again.
- The battery will automatically stop charging when fully charged. It is recommended to disconnect from the charger after fully charged.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions
My original fingertip remote controller broke and I bought a new one. How do I connect it to NAVI?
The new fingertip remote controller has not been paired with NAVI. For the first connection, you need to put the fingertip remote controller into pairing mode to establish a connection with NAVI. For the specific steps, refer to the “Initial Pairing Method” section of this manual.
When controlling NAVI with the fingertip remote controller, NAVI moves very slowly. How do I make it faster?
The fingertip remote controller has a high-speed mode and a low-speed mode; low-speed mode is the default. To increase speed, press the speed toggle button to switch modes. After increasing speed, please adapt gradually and avoid pushing the joystick too aggressively to prevent collisions.
The fingertip remote controller worked well at first, but sometimes it stops responding and then recovers after a moment.
The effective control range of the fingertip remote controller is 5 m, and up to 10 m in open areas. Nearby moving people or metal obstacles will significantly affect signal transmission and may cause disconnection. Once the distance meets the minimum communication range, the controller automatically reconnects to NAVI. For best results, use in open areas within 10 m.
