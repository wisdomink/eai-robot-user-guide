# 3. Quick Start Guide

## I. Startup

### Step 1: Open Client FF Robotics App

Open the official controller. The power button is located at the top. Long press the power button until the white light next to the power button lights up. After the controller is turned on, it will automatically open FF Robotics App and enter the robot startup wizard.

![Controller power button](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-1.png)

Select "FF Futurist" in the robot type selection interface, enter the boot wizard, and complete the startup operation according to the prompts.

### Step 2: Lift the robot

Before starting the robot, it needs to be lifted. Connect the robot to the hook on the top of the electric shifter through the lifting ring and cable on the shoulder, then lift the robot until its feet are completely off the ground.

Notes:

1. The lifting rings installed on the robot's shoulders must be securely fastened.
2. Both shoulder lifting rings must be connected before lifting the robot.
3. During lifting, avoid squeezing or hitting dexterous hands.
4. The lifting bracket is only for lifting the FF Futurist robot.
5. The lifting capacity is designed solely for hoisting the robot.

![Lift the robot](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-2.png)

After lifting the robot, click "Next" on the FF Robotics App interface.

### Step 3: Install the battery and turn on the robot

The battery is installed on the back of the robot. Confirm that the battery is fully charged, then slide the battery from top to bottom into the battery slot on the back of the robot until you hear a click.

![Install the battery](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-3.png)

Press the power button on the back of the robot waist to turn it on. When the robot power is connected, the power button lights up.

![Power button](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-4.png)

### Step 4: Wireless emergency stop

Upon receiving the emergency-stop remote control, it defaults to the released state, with the motor powered and operating normally.

In case of machine abnormality, pressing the remote button immediately cuts off the motor power, halting the machine for safety. After resolving the issue, rotating the button back to the released state restores power to the motor.

Notes:

1. The emergency-stop remote is pre-paired at the factory and ready to use out of the box.
2. The remote features a dual-channel design. Channel 1 is used to control motor power on and off; if channel 1 fails, it automatically switches to channel 2, which can only cut power.
3. When the emergency-stop remote is beyond the 10-meter operating range, the emergency-stop function may fail. Ensure it is operated within the normal range.

![Wireless emergency stop](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-5.png)

During robot joint self-inspection, the neck rotates in a small area, and the wrists and fingers swing to zero. After zero correction is completed, the wrists return to a vertical state and both hands make fists.

![Self-inspection complete](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-6.png)

After the robot joint self-inspection is completed, click "Next" on the FF Robotics App interface. 

### Step 5: Connect the robot

Click the "One-click Connect to Robot" button to establish a connection between the wireless controller and the robot.

For enhanced security, change the default password (02270227) upon first use to prevent unauthorized device connections.

![Connect to the robot](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-7.png)

After the robot is successfully connected, the FF Robotics App interface will display the words "Connection Successful", click "Next". 

### Step 6: Switch standing posture

Before the robot stands, it needs to be switched to the ready-standing posture in the suspended state. Click "Switch Standing Posture", and the robot automatically adjusts the position of the head, arms, and legs.

During the process of switching positions, the robot joints will move. Stay away from the robot and ensure there is sufficient space around it.

![Preparing to stand](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-8.png)

After completing the standing posture switch, click "Next" on the FF Robotics App interface. 

### Step 7: Lower the robot

Control the electric shifter to lower the robot until the feet fully touch the ground, the suspension rope is completely relaxed, and there is some margin.

![Robot's feet fully touch the ground](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-9.png)

After completing the robot lowering operation, click "Next" on the FF Robotics App interface. 

### Step 8: Switch to Standing Mode

Click "Enable Standing Mode", and the robot will enter standing mode. When you see "Standing Successful!", the robot has completed standing and can maintain autonomous balance standing.

Before switching to standing mode, make sure the robot has descended to the ground and its feet are fully in contact with the ground.

![Self-standing](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-10.png)

Click "Finish" to exit the boot wizard, and FF Robotics App will enter the workplace page.

## II. Shutdown

### Step 1: Safely stop the robot

Click the red "Stop" button at the bottom of the FF Robotics App sidebar to safely stop the robot, and the robot will enter the joint-freeze state.

### Step 2: Lift the robot

Install a lifting ring on the robot's shoulder and lift the robot using an electric shifter until the robot's feet are completely off the ground.

### Step 3: Shut down

Press the power button in the maintenance compartment at the back of the robot waist to power off the robot.

## III. Motion control

### III.I Preparation for walking

Before remotely controlling FF Futurist to walk, ensure that the robot has completed standing. After the robot stands up, click the "Time and Battery Area" in the upper right corner of the FF Robotics App main interface to bring up the Toolbox panel and view the corresponding RC Actions bar.

Double-click to switch to "walking" mode, and the robot starts to move in place.

### III.II Remote walking

Click the "Control" button on the software sidebar to enter the "Remote Walking" page. Press and hold the "Options" button in the upper right corner of the controller for 3 seconds to activate remote-control mode.

Controller button function description:

- Left joystick up: walk forward
- Left joystick down: walk backward
- Left joystick left: move horizontally to the left
- Left joystick right: move horizontally to the right
- Left joystick diagonal: move diagonally
- X "key: Turn left in place 
- B "key: Turn right in place 
- [Key combination] Left joystick + X/B keys: turn and walk 

Always maintain robot operation within the operator's direct line of sight.

### III.III Action display

Double-click the robot status in the RC Actions bar to switch to "Force Control Standing: Action Library Call".

Click "Interaction" in the sidebar to switch to the interaction page. Click "Action List" at the top of the screen to enter the action list page. Swipe up and down to view preset robot actions and click the play button on a card to play the action.

![Action List](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-11.png)

## IV. Charging & battery swapping

### IV.1 Charging

#### Step 1: Turn off the machine

Turn off the robot by pressing the power button in the maintenance compartment on the back side of the robot waist.

#### Step 2: Machine in hoisting-ready mode

Before powering up the robot, switch the robot to hoisting-ready mode.

#### Step 3: Open the maintenance compartment

Open the maintenance compartment cover behind the robot.

#### Step 4: Connect the charger

The charging port is located below the rear cover of the robot body waist. Connect the charging head of the power adapter to the charging port.

![Charging port](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-12.png)

### IV.2 Battery swapping

#### Step 1: Shut down

Refer to the shutdown process in the previous section.

#### Step 2: Machine in hoisting-ready mode

Before powering up the robot, switch the robot to hoisting-ready mode.

#### Step 3: Remove the battery

Press the square mechanical button on the top of the battery to unlock and lift the battery up to remove it.

#### Step 4: Install the battery

Refer to the startup battery installation process.

![Remove battery](/images/futurist-ultra/futurist-ultra-quick-start-guide-13.png)

Always maintain robot operation within the operator's direct line of sight.
