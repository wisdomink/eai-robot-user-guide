# Routine Maintenance

We need to carry out necessary maintenance of the robot to increase the life of the robot, so that the robot is in good condition for a long time and creates more value for customers.

- Before maintenance, the power must be turned off to prevent accidental electric shock or parts damage.
- The machine should be placed in a dry and ventilated place to prevent humid environment from affecting electronic and electrical components and shortening their lifespan.
