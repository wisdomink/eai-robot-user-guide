**RF exposure:**
The equipment complies with radiation exposure limits set for an uncontrolled environment. To avoid the possibility of exceeding radio frequency exposure limits, human proximity to the antenna shall not be less than 20 cm during normal operation. This transmitter must not be co-located or operated in conjunction with any antenna or transmitter.
