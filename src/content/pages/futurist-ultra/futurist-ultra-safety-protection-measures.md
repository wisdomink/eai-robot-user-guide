# 1. Safety Protection Measures

## 1.1 Safety Guide

To ensure safety, please comply with the following regulations when operating and maintaining the FF Futurist.

### 1.1.1 WARNING: Do not operate the robot

- Before using the robot, read the user manual carefully to understand the robot's functions, operating methods, and safety precautions.
- Use the robot on a level surface. Avoid extremely high or low temperatures, humid environments, steep slopes, large drops, fragile surfaces, and areas with vibration.
- Ensure that the robot has enough operating space. Avoid narrow or crowded environments to reduce the risk of collision and pinching.
- All robots are equipped with an emergency stop device. Users must be familiar with the location and use of the emergency stop during operation so that the robot can be stopped promptly in an emergency.
- Before operation, make sure all cables, plugs, and sockets are intact. In case of abnormal conditions such as short circuit or overheating, disconnect the power immediately.
- Perform regular maintenance and inspections to ensure that components such as joints, sensors, and power systems are operating normally.
- Prevent misuse. Ensure that the robot is not used in any improper, dangerous, or legally prohibited scenarios.

WARNING: Risk of personal injury. Sensors and controls shall only be serviced by a skilled person.

This equipment complies with part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions:

1. This device may not cause harmful interference.
2. This device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

### 1.1.2 Before turning on the robot

- Check there is enough battery to complete the task.
- Check the work area is free of people or other obstacles, especially near its moving parts.
- Check the components of the machine are in good condition.
- Check whether the emergency button is working properly.

### 1.1.3 Precautions during manual operation of the robot

- Maintain a safe distance of 4 to 6 feet (>=1.5m to 2m) from the robot's working range to avoid accidental collisions.
- Strictly adhere to the robot's designed load limits to avoid overloading.
- During robot operation, direct intervention in its actions is prohibited. If adjustments or manual intervention are needed, ensure the robot is completely stopped and powered off.
- If using the robot's remote operation function, ensure a secure network environment to prevent control errors due to network failure or external intrusion.
- During remote control, the operator should monitor the equipment in real time to ensure full awareness of the robot and surrounding environment.
- In emergencies, use the remote emergency-stop to stop the robot.
- Report for repair in time when the machine malfunctions.

### 1.1.4 When the task is complete

- Make sure to power off except for charging.
- Please store the machine in the designated location and do not put the machine on a slope.

### 1.1.5 Maintenance warning

- Use the spare parts specified by the manufacturer for replacement.
- Do not disassemble the machine on your own. Contact professional repair personnel designated by the manufacturer if repairs are required.
- Do not modify the machine by changing its original design or configuration. Any modification requires manufacturer authorization.

### 1.1.6 When transferring the robot

- Power off when transferring the machine.
- Do not touch the laser and other sensors directly, otherwise it will affect performance.
- If the machine is transported by vehicle, use packing belts, boxes, wooden plates, foam, and similar materials to protect the machine.
- Caution: risk of crushing the body.
- Caution: protective equipment is necessary when transferring the robot.

### 1.1.7 When the robot is not used for a long time

- Store the machine at a temperature of -4 to 20~70 degrees C.
- The machine needs to be fully charged at least once every 60 days, otherwise battery life will be affected.
- If the user does not use the machine for more than one week, ensure that the machine power is not less than 60% before shutdown, and turn off the main power switch.

## 1.2 Precautions for Use

- Ensure the robot is used on a flat surface (slope <=2%), avoiding extreme temperatures, humidity, steep slopes, uneven surfaces, fragile planes, and vibrating areas.
- Ensure the robot has sufficient operating space and that the operating area is physically separated from spectator areas by safety barriers.
- Strictly adhere to the robot's designed load limits to avoid overloading.
- During robot operation, direct intervention in its actions is prohibited. If manual intervention is needed, ensure the robot is completely stopped and powered off.
- Do not charge the battery if the battery is removed from this machine.
- In emergencies, use the remote emergency-stop to stop the robot.
- Report for repair in time when the machine malfunctions.
- CAUTION: this machine is for indoor use only.
- WARNING: to reduce the risk of fire, electric shock, or injury, do not use this machine in outdoor environments.
- CAUTION: this machine shall be stored indoors only.
- Using this machine as a toy is strictly prohibited. Extra attention is required when used by or near children.
- Use only as described in this manual. Use only attachments recommended by the manufacturer.

## 1.3 Maintenance and Management Guidelines

Robot

- Perform regular maintenance as recommended by FF AI-Robotics Inc. to ensure the robot is always in optimal working condition.
- If the robot malfunctions during operation, stop immediately and notify professional technicians for inspection and repair.
- Do not disassemble or repair the robot while it is running.
- If the robot uses a battery, ensure it is charged in a safe, dry environment.
- Avoid connecting the robot to power for extended periods to prevent overcharging or overheating.
- Ensure the operating system and control software are regularly updated to prevent vulnerabilities and security risks.
- This appliance contains batteries that are only replaceable by skilled persons.
- A charger that is suitable for one type of battery pack may create a risk of fire when used with another battery pack.
- For recharging the battery, only use the detachable supply unit `UY600L-W546080` provided with this appliance.
- Before maintenance, the emergency stop must be initiated first, then the robot must be hoisted by the equipped lifting device.
- Do not charge the battery pack or appliance outside of the temperature range specified in the instructions.
- The battery shall be removed from the appliance before it is scrapped, and the battery shall be disposed of safely.
- To restore factory settings, go to `Settings -> Restore` in FF Robotics App.
