## Non-Warranty Clause (Exclusions)

1. Damage caused by user secondary development
2. Damage caused by hardware modification
3. Unauthorized shell opening
4. Damage caused by force majeure or extreme environments
5. Appearance, structural, or internal circuit damage caused by improper use
6. Damage caused by collision and fall
7. Damage caused by liquid or foreign matter entering the robot
8. Data security accidents
9. Illegal use, including war activities, terrorism, or violation of laws and regulations
10. Improper use in Frenzy mode, including high-speed impact or special-action fall damage

## Warranty Support Services

### Remote technical support

1. Required materials: equipment serial number, fault description, and on-site image data
2. After receiving the request, technical support will diagnose the issue and provide the result and maintenance plan

### Maintenance support

1. Repair: Faulty equipment can be returned to the designated maintenance center. Warranty-covered product quality issues are exempt from labor and spare parts costs.
2. Maintenance period: 7 working days after receiving the equipment for repair, with additional communication required in special cases.
3. Logistics: Round-trip logistics costs are borne by the user whether insured or not, and logistics are designated by the company unless otherwise communicated.

### Exception Clause

1. The company shall not bear any third-party claims arising from product use in any form.
2. The company shall not be liable for data storage, loss, or leakage. Customers must back up data by themselves.
