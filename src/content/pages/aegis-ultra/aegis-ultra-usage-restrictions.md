# Usage Restrictions & Safety Notes

## Usage Restrictions

- This product must not be used by individuals with physical, sensory, or mental impairments, or by those lacking relevant experience and knowledge, including children, unless supervised by a responsible guardian.
- Do not allow children to treat this product as a toy. Supervise children and pets during operation to keep them at a safe distance.
- If the power cord is damaged, it must be replaced with a special cord or component obtained from the manufacturer or authorized service provider.
- Do not operate the product in elevated areas without protective barriers.
- If the product exhibits abnormal behavior such as leg flailing or violent shaking, press the emergency stop button to activate soft-stop safety mode.
- Do not place the product upside down and do not use the robot's legs as handles for carrying.
- Do not operate the product in environments above 40 C or below 0 C.
- Do not operate the product in areas with electromagnetic interference or heavy Wi-Fi / 2.4G interference.
- Before starting the product, ensure there is at least 1 meter of clear space around it.
- When the product is ascending or descending stairs or slopes, do not stand below it.
- Do not place children, pets, or objects on the robot whether it is moving or stationary.
- Do not insert hands into the range of motion of the robot's joints during transport.
- Power off the robot and unplug the power cord before cleaning or maintenance.
- Do not wipe the robot or charging dock with a wet cloth or rinse them with liquids.
- Do not use third-party batteries, power cords, or charging docks.

## Safety Notes

1. Joint and Cable Inspection: Check for foreign objects around the joints, inspect all cables for damage, and ensure the footpads are intact.
2. Battery Level and Camera Cleaning: Confirm sufficient battery level and keep cameras clean before operation.
3. Product Cleaning Caution: Do not use alcohol or corrosive solvents to clean this product.
4. Proper Placement and Ground Conditions: Place the robot on a hard, flat, and appropriate surface to avoid joint stress and body scratches.
5. Foot Pad Wear: Inspect foot pad wear regularly and replace when necessary.
6. Safe Operation and Terrain Adaptability: Keep the robot within your line of sight and maintain distance from obstacles and collision points.
7. Speed, Turning, and Complex Terrain: Avoid sharp or fast turns on steep, uneven, or unknown terrain.
8. Steep Slopes and Climbing Angles: Operate only within the specified climbing angle.
9. Maximum Speed and Prohibited Usage Environments: Do not use on slippery surfaces, cluttered areas, or terrain beyond specification.
10. Joint and Battery Condition Check: If joints are loose, the robot cannot stand, or excessive noise occurs, stop use immediately.
11. Low Battery Warning: Charge promptly when battery is low.
12. Safety Precautions: Avoid contact with moving parts and hot metal motor surfaces.
13. Heat Dissipation and Cleaning: Allow the robot to rest periodically during prolonged operation.
14. Power Supply Specifications: Use only fast-charging cables that meet the product specifications.
15. Shutdown Notice: Power off the robot immediately after use.
