# Troubleshooting

| Frequently Asked Questions | Treatment Measure |
| --- | --- |
| What if the remote control cannot connect to the robot? | Confirm that the robot has started and no other version of the app is running in the background. Restart the robot and app, then try again. |
| What if the connected controller has no response? | Make sure the controller is connected to the correct Wi-Fi. If connected but still uncontrollable, wait 10 seconds and try again. If there is still no response, restart the robot. |
| Is it normal for the robot to stop moving on its own? | The protection function may have been triggered. Wait for 10 minutes and try again. If movement still cannot be resumed, check battery level and controller signal. |

## Abnormal Conditions

The size of the packing box is `80cm x 45cm x 25cm`. Before carrying the box, please remove the battery from the robot. During transportation, keep the front of the box facing up.

## Storage Environment

When storing the power adapter and the entire unit, place them in a dry and cool environment away from direct sunlight, fire sources, and strong electromagnetic fields. The recommended storage temperature is between 20 C and 25 C.

Do not store the product in environments exceeding 60 C or below -10 C.

## Battery Precautions

Long idle time may affect battery performance. Avoid deep discharge to maintain battery state. The battery will stop charging automatically when fully charged, but it is recommended to disconnect it after charging.

Check the battery charge regularly and perform charge / discharge maintenance every 3 months. If the charge is less than 20%, charge it to 40%-60% for storage to avoid battery damage.
