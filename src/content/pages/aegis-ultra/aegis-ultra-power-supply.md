**Battery Display in on State**

| No. | SOC (%) | LED 1 | LED 2 | LED 3 | LED 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 – 14% | Flashing | OFF | OFF | OFF |
| 2 | 15 – 24% | ON | OFF | OFF | OFF |
| 3 | 25 – 49% | ON | ON | OFF | OFF |
| 4 | 50 – 74% | ON | ON | ON | OFF |
| 5 | 75 – 100% | ON | ON | ON | ON |
