# Product Overview & Components
 
**FX Aegis Ultra**
- 12 degrees of freedom — full-body articulated quadruped robot.
- Supports multiple locomotion gaits and motion behaviors.
- Open SDK for motion control algorithm development and communication protocols.
- Enables users to conduct secondary development and custom integrations.

**Component Highlights**
- FPV Camera — forward-facing for real-time video
- Taillight — status indicator
- Expansion Interface — modular payload support
- Anti-Collision Head & Tail Hammers — impact protection
- Replaceable Battery — hot-swap capable
- 12 Joints — 3 per leg (hip, thigh, shin)
- Anti-Slip Foot Pads — terrain grip
- Protective Rubber Pads — body protection
