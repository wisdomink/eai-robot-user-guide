# Preparation Before First Use

## Start Up

- Remove the robot from the packaging box and place it on a flat surface.
- Take out the battery and insert it into the battery compartment.
- Press the battery power button briefly once, then press and hold for 5 seconds to power on.
- Wait for the boot sequence to complete. Blue LED steady means boot complete.

## Shut Down

- Ensure the robot is in a lying-down position.
- Press and hold the power button for over 3 seconds.
- Once all LEDs on the battery have turned off, the device is in shutdown state.

## Emergency Stop

- Press `L1 + R1` simultaneously on the remote controller, or use the Emergency Stop option in the app.
- The robot will activate soft-stop mode and automatically lie down.
- Resume normal operation only when it is safe to do so.
