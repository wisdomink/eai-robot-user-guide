# Product Overview & Components

## FX Aegis Ultra

- 12 degrees of freedom full-body articulated quadruped robot
- Supports multiple locomotion gaits and motion behaviors
- Open SDK for motion control algorithm development and communication protocols
- Enables secondary development and custom integrations

## Component Highlights

- FPV Camera for forward real-time video
- Taillight for status indication
- Expansion Interface for modular payload support
- Anti-Collision Head and Tail Hammers for impact protection
- Replaceable battery with hot-swap capability
- 12 joints, 3 per leg
- Anti-slip foot pads for terrain grip
- Protective rubber pads for body protection
