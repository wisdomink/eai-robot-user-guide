**Connection method**
1. Remove the battery and insert it into the battery compartment of the robot dog. Press briefly.
Press the battery power button once, then hold it down for 5 seconds. Release it and wait for 20 seconds. The startup is now complete. At this point, the battery indicator light shows the remaining battery power of the robot dog. 

![Remote controller top view](/images/aegis-edu/remote-control-intro-2.png)

2. Check above the remote control to ensure it is in the X position.

3. Press LB+Y to make the robot dog stand up and gently push the left joystick forward to try to control it. If the robot dog does not respond when you move it forward, press the MODE key and then gently push the left joystick forward.

Warning: Stunt actions may affect the lifespan of the device. 

· Regular mode: Features stable regular movement capabilities and standard actions, suitable for daily use. 

· Laboratory Mode: Enhance athletic performance and unlock more stunts. 

 
Stunt actions in Lab Mode put a lot of strain on the joints. Using it for too long can cause faster wear and cause faster wear and shorten the device's life. Use with care and keep sessions short to maintain the device's health.
