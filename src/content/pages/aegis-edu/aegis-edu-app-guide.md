# APP User Guide

## I. APP Download
Click the link http://www.zhiyuan-robot.com/DOCS/SW/Dl/app to download the AgibotGO APP.

## II. Account Registration and Login
Register and log in to the APP using your mobile phone number or email address.

![APP download](/images/aegis-edu/app-download-1.png)

## III. Equipment Connection 
### 1. Click the "Add Robot" button
![Account registration](/images/aegis-edu/app-download-2.png)

### 2. Select to add a robot 
![Add robot](/images/aegis-edu/app-download-3.png)

### 3. Select the network connection mode. Two modes are supported: WiFi and direct connection to AP.
![Select robot](/images/aegis-edu/app-download-4.png)

### 4. Select WiFi connection mode Automatically read the current Wifi network connected to the mobile phone.

**Connecting**

**Connection successful. Return to the APP home page.**

### 5. Select AP connection mode
![AP connection mode](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-2.png)

## Operation page

1. The upper left corner can be used to switch between mobile mode and stationary mode;
![Stationary mode](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-5.png)

2. In mobile mode, the left joystick controls the robot's walking direction, and the right joystick controls the robot's rotation direction. 

3. In place mode, the joystick operation is switched to left and right head movement and pitch. The robot cannot walk in place mode.
![Emergency stop](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-6.png)

4. Press the red emergency stop button to trigger the emergency stop operation. 
![Stunt moves](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-7.png)

5. Click on the left to perform a forward jump, an upward jump, or a backflip. The prompts indicate these are stunt moves.
![Speed setting](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-8.png)

6. Robot speed setting
![Forward jump](/images/aegis-edu/app-operations-9.png)

**Warning: Stunt actions may affect the lifespan of the device.**

**Performing stunt actions will impose a significant load on the joint modules of the equipment. Prolonged operation may lead to accelerated wear of the joints, seriously affecting their service life.
Life. Please open it carefully and control the usage time reasonably to ensure the long-term stability of the device.**
