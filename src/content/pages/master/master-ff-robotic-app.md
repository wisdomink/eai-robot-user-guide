# 3.7 FF Robotic APP Manual

FF Robotic APP is the official companion control application for the FF Master robot.

Through FF Robotic APP, users can perform full lifecycle management of the robot, including:

- Network configuration and connection
- Motion control and posture management
- OTA firmware updates and version upgrades
- Exhibition and showroom navigation modes

## 3.7.1 APP Download

The latest version of the app corresponds to the latest firmware version of the robot. Make sure to download and use the correct version.

## 3.7.2 Remote Controller operations

Functions include checking controller status, re-pairing the controller, and deleting historical pairing records.

### Re-pair the Controller

The remote controller is pre-configured and paired at the factory. No manual re-pairing is required during normal use.

![Default pairing](/images/master/master-ff-robotic-app-1.png)

If you need to re-pair or connect another controller:

- Press and hold both buttons inside the red box at the bottom of the controller.
- Wait until the blue indicator light starts flashing to indicate pairing mode.

![Repairing](/images/master/master-ff-robotic-app-2.png)

### Delete Pairing History

- If historical connection records remain, pressing the marked button at startup will reconnect automatically.
- If history has been deleted, you need to enter pairing mode again.

![Automatic pairing](/images/master/master-ff-robotic-app-3.png)

Connection notes:

- Each controller has a unique MAC address.
- In the future app Bluetooth scan interface, the MAC address is displayed beneath the device name.
- If automatic reconnect fails, wait briefly or enter pairing mode again.

### 3.7.3.1 Safety Notes

1. When the robot switches to full-body motion control mode, pay close attention to its movements and provide manual support or protective measures in advance.
2. Avoid triggering passive mode, emergency-stop mode, or damping mode unless necessary.
