# 3.4 Charging Procedure

## 3.4.1 Charging

1. Charging Method Selection

- Method 1: After powering off the robot, directly connect the charger plug to the device charging port.
- Method 2: After powering off the robot, remove the battery first, then insert the charger plug into the battery charging port.

2. Magnetic Charging Port Operation Notes

- The charging port uses a magnetic design. Bring the charger plug close to the charging port and it will automatically align.
- During charging, make sure the magnetic charging port is fully aligned to avoid poor contact.

![Magnetic charging port location](/images/master/master-charging-procedure-1.png)

Do not charge the robot while it is powered on. During charging, do not use the remote controller or operate the robot in other ways.

## 3.4.2 Battery Replacement

- Power off: Refer to the shutdown procedure.
- Remove the battery: Pull out the battery handle fixed on the front of the battery pack, insert your fingers into the handle loop, and pull outward to remove the battery.
- Install the battery: Follow the installation procedure described in the startup section.
