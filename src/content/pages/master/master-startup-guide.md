# 3.2 Start-Up Guide

## 3.2.1 Start-Up with Hoisting (Gantry Needed)

### Step 1: Hoist the Robot Body

- Open the package and slowly lift the robot out.
- Use the protective lifting frame to suspend the robot naturally, ensuring that the feet do not touch the ground.

![Step1-1 Unbox](/images/master/master-startup-guide-1.png)
![Step1-2 Carefully Lift Out the Robot](/images/master/master-startup-guide-2.png)
![Step1-3 Suspend the robot naturally](/images/master/master-startup-guide-3.png)

### Step 2: Install the Battery

- Insert the battery into the rear battery slot.
- Push it down until you hear a click, then press lightly again to confirm it is locked.

![Install battery](/images/master/master-startup-guide-4.png)

### Step 3: Power On

- Ensure the battery level is at least two bars.
- Short press the power button to wake the system, then long press for 5 seconds to power on.

![Power on](/images/master/master-startup-guide-5.png)

### Step 4: Switch to Standing Preparation Mode

- After initialization, lower the suspension ropes until both feet fully contact the ground.
- Press `[L2 + X]` on the remote controller to activate standing preparation mode.

![Feet contact ground](/images/master/master-startup-guide-6.png)
![Standing preparation mode](/images/master/master-startup-guide-7.png)

### Step 5-7

- Slowly lower the robot until both feet are fully in contact with the ground.
- Press `[R2 + X]` to enter stable standing mode.
- Use the left and right sticks to enter locomotion mode and move.

![Stable standing mode](/images/master/master-startup-guide-8.png)

## 3.2.2 Start-Up from Supine Position (No Gantry Needed)

- Unbox and place the robot in folded posture.
- Install the battery and power on.
- Adjust the robot to a supine position with face upward and keep at least 0.5m of clear area around it.
- Short press `[Up + Triangle]` to trigger the supine-to-stand motion.

![Supine unbox 1](/images/master/master-startup-guide-9.png)
![Supine unbox 2](/images/master/master-startup-guide-10.png)
![Supine folded posture](/images/master/master-startup-guide-11.png)
![Supine battery install](/images/master/master-startup-guide-12.png)
![Supine power on](/images/master/master-startup-guide-13.png)
![Supine position ready](/images/master/master-startup-guide-14.png)
![Supine to stand](/images/master/master-startup-guide-15.png)

Notes:

- Supine-to-stand is not supported when dexterous hands or grippers are attached.
- Place the robot on a flat, hard, level surface before the motion.

## 3.2.3 Start-up from Prone Position (No Gantry Device Required)

- Unbox and place the robot face-down on a level surface.
- Install the battery and power on.
- Press `[Up + Triangle]` simultaneously to make the robot rise from prone position to standing.

## 3.2.4 Start-up from Sitting Position (No Gantry Device Required)

- Unbox and place the robot in folded posture.
- Install the battery and power on.
- Adjust the robot to a sitting position on a stable 35-40 cm high platform or bench.
- Short press `[Up + X]` to enter sitting preparation mode.
- Short press `[Up + Square]` to execute sit-to-stand.

![Sitting unbox 1](/images/master/master-startup-guide-22.png)
![Sitting unbox 2](/images/master/master-startup-guide-23.png)
![Sitting folded posture](/images/master/master-startup-guide-24.png)
![Sitting battery install](/images/master/master-startup-guide-25.png)
![Sitting power on](/images/master/master-startup-guide-26.png)
![Sitting preparation](/images/master/master-startup-guide-27.png)
![Standing position](/images/master/master-startup-guide-28.png)

Notes:

- Before standing up, ensure the robot is securely placed on a stable 35-40 cm platform.
- Confirm both feet are fully in contact with the ground before executing the sit-to-stand motion.
