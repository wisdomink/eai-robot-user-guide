# 3.1 Safety Precautions

1. Transportation and Lifting Safety: When transporting or lifting the robot, avoid compression or collision to prevent structural damage or safety hazards.

2. Ground Environment Requirements: Place the robot on a hard, flat, and non-slippery surface. The ground material should meet a static friction coefficient greater than 0.4.

3. Operating Space Reservation: Reserve a safety area with a radius of at least 1 meter around the robot. Personnel should not stand within this area.

4. Robot Battery Management: When powered by the battery, closely monitor battery status. Once battery power is cut off, the robot may lose power and fall.

5. Remote Controller Power Management: Ensure the remote controller has sufficient power. Low power may prevent normal mode switching and cause unexpected tipping.

6. Mode Usage Restriction: Under non-emergency conditions, do not activate emergency stop mode or damping mode, as these modes disable joint torque and may cause the robot to fall.
