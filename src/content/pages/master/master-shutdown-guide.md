# 3.3 Shutdown Guide

## 3.3.1 Hoisted Shutdown (Gantry Needed)

1. Install the hoisting ring on the back of the robot and perform initial hoisting.
2. Press `[L2 + X]` to switch to standing preparation mode.
3. Recommended safe procedure:

- Short press `[L2 + R2 + Create]` to switch to damping mode.
- Then short press `[L1 + R1 + Create]` to switch to zero-torque mode.

4. Slowly hoist the robot until both feet are completely off the ground.
5. Short press the power button to wake the device, then long press for about 5 seconds to power off.
6. Repack the robot in the box.

## 3.3.2 Assisted Lying-Down Shutdown (No Gantry Required)

1. Short press `[L2 + X]` to enter standing-preparation mode.
2. Manually support the robot and gently lay it flat on the ground.
3. After confirming the robot is fully flat, switch first to damping mode and then to zero-torque mode.
4. Power off by long-pressing the battery power button.
5. Repack the robot.

## 3.3.3 Sitting-Position Shutdown (No Gantry Required)

1. Short press `[L2 + Left]` to make the robot perform the sitting motion.
2. Once the robot is in a stable sitting posture, switch to zero-torque mode using the recommended safe procedure.
3. Long press the power button to power off.
4. Repack the robot.

- During sitting shutdown, support the back handle or keep the robot leaning against the backrest to maintain balance and prevent tipping.
