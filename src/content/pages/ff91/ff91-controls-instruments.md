#### Low State of Charge (SoC) Notifications

If the SoC falls below normal levels while driving, the vehicle will implement a three-stage notification to the driver.

![](images/ffom0187.png)

When a low battery SoC (< 21%) is detected, the meter and estimated range are shaded in red.

> **WARNING:** Just before the SoC falls to 0%, the red error icon will flash alongside the speedometer. This indicates the vehicle will shut down within 60 seconds. DO NOT PANIC. Slow down and pull off the road safely, and contact Roadside Assistance. See [Contacting Roadside Assistance].

![](images/ffom0191.png)

> **NOTE:** The battery has a small amount of energy remaining after SoC = 0% is reached. This allows for a small driving range of a few miles depending on environmental factors. Driving the vehicle in this region could reduce battery lifetime. Additionally, charging current is reduced in this SoC region and AC/DC charging will take longer when battery is discharged below SoC = 0%.

> **NOTE:** At low temperature the SOC might drop faster, since the useable battery energy is limited by cold temperature. Charge your vehicle regularly in cold conditions.

### STARTING & POWERING OFF


#### Approach unlock and power-up

The vehicle is configured to automatically wake up when a valid key is detected. Typically this sequence is as follows:

- User approaches vehicle with bluetooth-enabled device from 30 feet (10 meters) and vehicle systems wake up. Once within 9 feet (3 meters) of the vehicle, the vehicle unlocks.
- Vehicle is driven.
- User leaves vehicle carrying bluetooth-enabled device. Once outside 9 feet (10 meters) of the vehicle, the vehicle auto-locks.
- If the bluetooth-enabled device is switched off during the journey, the vehicle will not auto-lock. A warning is displayed on the Central Information Display. See [Phone key not detected].
- If a bluetooth-enabled device isn’t available, the NFC card can be used to open, drive and lock the vehicle instead. See [Using the NFC Card].

---


#### Starting

The vehicle powers up to a driving mode (**READY**) once a valid NFC key or Bluetooth-enabled device is detected in range of the vehicle and the vehicle is unlocked.

##### Driving mode

![](images/ffom0110.png)

The **READY** indicator illuminates on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD) whenever Driving mode is available. 

##### Phone key not detected

This message is displayed if a valid phone key is not detected. If a paired device fails to wake the vehicle: 

- Check that Bluetooth is enabled on the device.
- Check that the paired device is within 9 feet (3 meters) of the vehicle.
- Check that the FFID is valid for the vehicle.

##### Phone key no longer detected

This message is displayed if the device used to unlock the vehicle is no longer detected within the key cycle. This may be because:

- The device has run out of power.
- The device has been switched off.
- The device has been removed from the vicinity of the vehicle.

To lock the vehicle after this message is displayed, switch on the relevant device or use an NFC card. See [Using the NFC Card].

---


#### Powering Off

Once the user’s paired device is 9 feet (3 meters) from the vehicle with no occupants present, the vehicle will lock. Once the paired device is 30 feet (10 meters) from the vehicle, and the vehicle has no occupants, the vehicle systems automatically power off

### BRAKES


#### Braking Systems

> **WARNING:** It is critical to occupant safety that your braking systems are always functioning properly. If you experience any issues with the brake pedal or receive any fault messages regarding the braking system, contact Faraday Future immediately.

> **WARNING:** Driving through heavy rain or water can have an adverse effect on braking efficiency. It is recommended that you lightly apply the brakes intermittently in order to dry the brakes.

> **WARNING:** Do not rest your foot on the brake pedal while the vehicle is in motion unless you are applying the brake pedal.

The foot pedal hydraulically-operated brakes are vacuum-assisted, but only when the vehicle is On. If the vehicle loses power when driving, you will need to apply more pressure on the brake pedal and you will experience longer stopping distances.

If the brake fluid level in the reservoir is low, a red brake indicator (telltale) will display on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD). See [Brake Fluid].

![Icon Brake Warning](images/icon_brake_warning.png)  The red brake indicator (telltale) displays on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD), when a brake system fault has been detected. Contact Faraday Future Service Support as soon as possible to have the brakes inspected.

The Instrument Panel Display (IPD) displays a ‘Failed Ibooster’ or ‘Failed Electronic Brake Distribution’ message if any faults are detected.

---


#### Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS)

> **WARNING:** Always maintain an appropriate stopping distance from the vehicle in front. ABS cannot overcome the physical limitations of trying to stop the vehicle in too short a distance, cornering at too high a speed, or the danger of hydroplaning, i.e. where a layer of water prevents adequate contact between the tires and the road surface.

> **WARNING:** The braking distance on road surfaces that are wet, slippery or loose is always increased; even for vehicles equipped with ABS.

> **WARNING:** Always drive with due care and attention to your surroundings and road conditions. ABS will not correct driver errors.

Your vehicle is equipped with an Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) that prevents the wheels from locking during hard braking or when braking on roads with reduced grip.

During braking, the ABS monitors the speed of each wheel and varies the brake fluid pressure at each wheel to prevent the wheels from locking. This system helps maintain steering ability during maximum brake application.

When ABS activates, you may experience the following conditions:

- Pulsations in the brake pedal
- A slight drop of the brake pedal
- Clicking or grinding noises
- ABS warning indicator flickering on and off as the system activates

These conditions demonstrate that the ABS is operating and are not a cause for concern. Maintain a firm and steady pressure on the brake pedal while experiencing the pulsation.

##### Emergency braking

> **WARNING:** Do not pump the brake pedal. This interrupts the operation of the ABS system and increases your stopping distance, which could lead to a collision.

In an emergency, fully press the brake pedal even when the road surface is slippery.

##### ABS warning indicator

The ABS indicator (telltale) displays on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD), along with a notification message.

- If the indicator is flashing, a fault has been detected.
- If the indicator is shown solid, the system has been disabled.

Contact Faraday Future as soon as possible to have the fault repaired.

> **CAUTION:** If the ABS warning indicator is displayed, the foot-operated braking system will remain operational. Be aware that braking distances may increase and wheels may lock under heavy braking.

---


#### Regenerative Braking

Whenever the vehicle is moving and your foot is off the accelerator, regenerative braking slows the vehicle and feeds energy back to the high-voltage battery.

While you should still use the brake pedal whenever needed to stop safely, you can take advantage of regenerative braking by anticipating your stops and reducing or removing pressure from the accelerator pedal to slow down.

> **NOTE:** If regenerative braking is aggressively slowing your vehicle (such as when your foot is completely off the accelerator pedal at highway speeds), the brake lights turn on to alert others that you are slowing down.

![](images/ffomss0017.png)

The green meter below the speedometer shows the current amount of energy being generated and fed back to the high-voltage battery.

The amount of generated energy being fed back to the high-voltage battery can vary depending on the current state of the battery. For example, regenerative braking may be limited if the battery is extremely hot or cold or if the battery is already charged to its maximum allowable level.

![](images/ffom0093.png)

It may also be engaged as follows:

> **CAUTION:** Driving the vehicle with the parking brake applied, or repeated use of the parking brake to slow the vehicle, may cause serious damage to the brake system.

### RIDE CONTROL SYSTEMS


#### Electronic Stability Control (ESC)

> **WARNING:** No electronic system can remove the need for safe driving practices. Although the Lucid Stability Control system can help to maintain control of the vehicle, it cannot prevent any accident which may result from the vehicle turning at too high a speed or from careless/dangerous driving techniques.

The Electronic Stability Control (ESC) system uses various sensors to monitor driver inputs and vehicle motion. Under certain driving conditions, the system helps to perform the following functions:

- Controls brake pressure to reduce wheel slip on one slipping drive wheel so power is transferred to a drive wheel on the same axle that is not slipping.
- Controls brake pressure and traction motor output to reduce drive wheel slip based on vehicle speed.
- Controls brake pressure at individual wheels and traction motor output to help the driver maintain control of the vehicle in the following conditions:
  - Under-steering
  - Over-steering
 **NOTE:**The ESC system cannot be disabled.

![Icon Esc](images/icon_esc.png)  If the ESC system operates while driving, the indicator (telltale) will flash on the Instrument Cluster (IPD).

#### Washers

> **WARNING:** Operating the washers in cold weather could cause the fluid to freeze on the windshield, obscuring your vision and causing a collision. Use the windshield heater to warm the windshield to reduce the possibility of the fluid freezing.

![](images/ffom0098.png)

### HEATING, VENTILATION & AIR CONDITIONING


#### Climate Controls

Cabin heating, ventilation, and air conditioning is divided into four zones: one for each seating position. 

These zones can be adjusted simultaneously or individually by using the touchscreen.

> **NOTE:** The door touchscreens can also control the individual climate for that zone.

To access the climate controls, press the ![](images/ffom0113.png) icon at the bottom of the touchscreen.

![](images/ffomss0021.png)

Press a seating position button to adjust for that zone, or press to activate **Sync** and adjust for all zones.

> **NOTE:** If a passenger adjusts a control via a door touchscreen while **Sync** is On, this turns **Sync** Off. The control states for the remaining zones stay as they were previously set while in **Sync**.

##### Climate On/Off

Press the ![](images/ffom0116.png) button to turn climate controls On or Off for an individual zone (or all zones when **Sync** is On).

> **NOTE:** When **climate** controls are On, the current fan speed and temperature settings of the Driver zone will be noted at the bottom of the screen, even if you switch to a different menu.

##### Steering wheel heat

Press the ![](images/icon_steering_wheel_heat.png) button to activate or deactivate steering wheel heat.

##### Air recirculation

Press the **Air-recirculation** button to switch between air circulation modes. Cabin air can be continuously recirculated, or fresh air can be drawn in from outside. When Air-recirculation is set to Auto, the vehicle chooses the recirculation mode based on exterior air quality and temperatures to provide optimum cabin air quality and minimize energy consumption.

> **NOTE:** Avoid recirculating cool interior air for a long time, as this can fog up the windows. Auto Air-recirculation is recommended for optimal cabin comfort and visibility.

##### Airflow direction

Press the ![](images/ffom0117.png)![](images/ffom0118.png)![](images/ffom0119.png) buttons to turn the fan for that area On or Off. Multiple areas can be selected simultaneously.

When the **Auto** button is On, fan speed is automated by the system based on the set temperature.

##### Air temperature

Use the center slider to adjust the air temperature. The current temperature setting for the selected zone is noted at the top of the slider.

Press the **A/C** button to turn air conditioning On or Off.

##### Seat heating and cooling

Touch and drag the right slider to ![](images/icon_seat_heat.png) raise or ![](images/icon_seat_cooling.png) lower the seat temperature up or down. Three levels of both heating and ventilation are available, with the central position being **Off**.
