## ADVANCED DRIVER ASSISTANCE SYSTEMS (ADAS)


### OVERVIEW


#### ADAS Menu

![](images/ffom0184.png)

> **WARNING:** Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) features are not a substitute for careful driving. The driver is responsible for control of the vehicle at all times.

Select the ADAS menu by pressing the **Settings** tab then **Driver Assist** on the left side.

1. Automatic Emergency Braking: If the AEB system detects an imminent collision, it will apply the brakes to mitigate any potential impact. Toggle the AEB system on or off using the switch. See [Automatic Emergency Braking].
2. Forward Collision Warning: If the vehicle detects a potential collision scenario, it will alert the driver audibly and visually. The FCW sensitivity can be switched between **Early**, **Mid** or **Late**, or switched off altogether.
3. Lane Departure Warning: Provides an audible and visual warning of the vehicle drifting out of detected lane markings. LDW is enabled or disabled with the switch. See [Lane Departure Warning].
4. Lane Keep Assist: Works in conjunction with LDW to maintain course between detected lane markings. Toggle LKA with the switch. See [Lane Keep Assist].
5. Traffic Sign Recognition: The vehicle recognizes standard road signs and displays a reminder of them on the instrument cluster. Toggle TSR on or off with the switch. See [Traffic Sign Recognition].

### DRIVING ASSISTANCE


#### High Beam Auto-Dimming

> **WARNING:** High Beam Auto-Dimming is an aid for selecting the best possible lighting based on prevailing conditions. The driver is always responsible for manually switching between high and low beam when traffic situations or weather conditions require this.

> **WARNING:** Do not use the High Beam Auto Dimming in the following situations: poor visibility, fog, heavy rain, snow irrespective if the High Beam Auto Dimming status appears to be operating or the High Beam Auto Dimming is not reporting an error.

> **WARNING:** You may need to override the system when approaching other road users.

> **WARNING:** You may need to override the system during inclement weather.

> **WARNING:** The system does not relieve you of your responsibility to drive with due care and attention. You may need to override the system if it does not turn the high beams on or off.

High Beam Auto Dimming control turns the high beams on if it is dark enough and no other traffic is present. If it detects an approaching vehicle's headlamps or tail lamps, or street lighting ahead, the system turns the high beams off.

The system turns the high beams on if all of the following occur:

- High Beam Auto Dimming feature is turned ON
- Low Beam is turned to Auto
- High Beam stalk is pushed to the high beam position
- The ambient light level is low enough to require high beam
- There is no traffic in front of the vehicle
- The vehicle speed is greater than 25 mph (40 km/h) and less than 84 mph (135 km/h)

Push the left-side stalk forward to enable auto high beam. Pull the stalk back to disable auto high beam. 

> **NOTE:** Pulling the stalk back from the neutral position operates the high beam flash.

![](images/ffom0096.png)

> **NOTE:** The feature may also detect street lighting.

The system turns the high beams off if any of the following occur:

- High Beam Auto Dimming is turned off
- Low beam is not turned to AUTO
- High Beam stalk is in the neutral position
- The ambient light level is high enough to not require high beam
- The system detects an approaching vehicle’s head lamps or tail lamps
- The system detects severe rain, snow or fog
- The system detects street lighting
- The camera has reduced visibility
- The vehicle speed falls below approximately 15 mph (25 km/h)

The high beams may not turn off automatically if the system fails to detect another vehicle's lamps due to the conditions listed below. High-Beam Auto Dimming should be disabled if any of the following conditions exist:

-  The other vehicle's lamps are missing, damaged, obstructed from view, or otherwise undetected
-  The other vehicle's lamps are covered with dirt, snow, and/or road spray
-  The other vehicle's lamps cannot be detected due to dense exhaust, smoke, fog, snow, road spray, mist, or other airborne obstructions
-  The vehicle's windshield is dirty, cracked, or obstructed by something that blocks the view of the light sensor
-  The vehicle is loaded such that the front end points upward, causing the light sensor to aim high and not detect headlamps and taillamps
-  The vehicle is being driven on winding or hilly roads

To activate High Beam Auto-Dimming, set the low beams to Auto mode, then push the left steering column lever away from you.

![Icon High Beam](images/icon_high_beam.png)  The High Beam Auto-Dimming indicator (telltale) is displayed on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD) whenever the system is activated.

- Blue icon with A: High beams are on and high beam auto dimming feature is on and ready to assist.
- Empty blue icon: High beams are on but high beam auto dimming feature is OFF.
- Grey icon with A: High beams are turned off because high beam auto dimming feature is active.

![Icon Bulb Failure-01](images/icon_bulb_failure-01.png)  If the system detects a fault, an indicator (telltale) is displayed on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD), and High-Beam Auto-Dimming is unavailable. Headlight High Beam can still be activated manually via the left steering column lever.

High Beam Auto-Dimming can be enabled or disabled via the **Lights** menu on the touchscreen.

#### Adaptive Cruise Control

> **WARNING:** Adaptive cruise control is a driver aid only. Always drive safely with due care and attention. Safe driving remains the responsibility of the driver.

> **WARNING:** Adaptive cruise control function can be impaired in certain weather and road conditions. Do not use adaptive cruise control in poor visibility conditions, including mist, hail, heavy rain, spray, or snow. Using adaptive cruise control in poor weather conditions increases the risk of a loss of control of the vehicle.

> **WARNING:** Adaptive cruise control cannot detect pedestrians in the roadway. Adaptive cruise control may not react to stationary or slow-moving vehicles and oncoming vehicles in the same lane. Be aware of these limitations when using adaptive cruise control.

> **WARNING:** The adaptive cruise control system uses a radar sensor to sense traffic ahead of the vehicle. The radar sensor is mounted at the front of the vehicle. Ensure this area of the vehicle is clean and free from obstructions such as debris, mud, snow, ice or stickers. If this area of the vehicle is obstructed, the adaptive cruise control system cannot detect hazards.

> **WARNING:** The driver should remain alert and intervene, if necessary, when adaptive cruise control is in operation. The adaptive cruise control system may operate unexpectedly in certain conditions. For example:

- If the vehicle in front chooses a different path.
- If a vehicle partially moves into the same lane. Adaptive cruise control can only detect a vehicle when it is fully in the same lane.
- When entering or exiting curves, vehicles in front may not be detected.
- When moving around a stationary vehicle, the system may not be able to discern which vehicle should be followed.
- When the vehicle in front moves out of the same lane, the system may not be able to discern which vehicle should be followed.

##### Adaptive Cruise Control Overview

Adaptive cruise control allows the driver to choose a preferred cruising speed. This cruising speed automatically reduces if a slower moving vehicle is detected ahead. Once a slower moving vehicle is detected, the a constant gap is maintained to the vehicle ahead. Once the system detects no vehicles are ahead, the vehicle accelerates to the preferred cruising speed. Adaptive cruise control brings the car to a stop when following traffic which slows to a halt.

> **NOTE:** Adaptive cruise control illuminates the brake lights when the brakes are applied

The Adaptive Cruise Control system can be switched on and off via the driver assistance menu. See [ADAS Menu].

#### Lane Keep Assist

> **WARNING:** The lane keep assist system is a driver aid only. The driver is fully responsible for ensuring the vehicle is driven safely. The driver must observe and respect local road laws and restrictions.

Lane Keep Assist (LKA) detects road lane division markings to assist in maintaining the current lane trajectory. If the system detects the vehicle is about to cross a lane boundary, steering torque is applied to correct the vehicle path to maintain position in the current lane.

> **NOTE:** The LKA system cannot assist the driver if road lane markings tracking is not available.

The Lane Keep Assist (LKA) system can be switched on and off via the driver assistance menu. See [ADAS Menu].

#### Traffic Sign Recognition

> **WARNING:** The traffic sign recognition system is for assisting the driver only. All responsibility remains with the driver to observe and respect local road laws and restrictions.

The traffic sign recognition system uses a forward-facing camera behind the windshield in conjunction with navigation map data. The camera detects speed limit signs, no overtaking signs, and variable overhead speed signs. The instrument cluster relays recognized traffic sign information. Where speed limit signs are not detected, the instrument panel defaults to information stored within the navigation data.

##### Traffic Sign Recognition limitations

The traffic sign recognition system is restricted if the windshield camera becomes blocked, e.g. by dirt, snow or debris. The traffic sign recognition system continues to display information from the vehicle’s navigation data. 

Traffic sign recognition: 

- Performance can be impaired by adverse weather, e.g. heavy fog, rain, snow or hail.
- May not perform correctly if the vehicle is traveling through areas not covered by the navigation system.
- May not recognize broken, concealed, covered, or non-standard road signs.
- Function may be impaired by bright lighting.
- May display a message if the windshield camera is blocked.

### COLLISION PROTECTION


#### Automatic Emergency Braking

> **WARNING:** Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) is a driver aid. Responsibility remains with the driver to drive with due care and attention, in a safe manner for any occupants and other road users. Always observe road signs and markings. Always act appropriately to any potential hazards.

> **WARNING:** AEB uses cameras and radars to detect hazards in front of the vehicle. AEB cannot detect every single object which the vehicle may encounter.

> **WARNING:** Correct AEB operation relies on the system detecting a clear image of traffic objects and an understanding of their movement. AEB cannot function if one or other of these parameters cannot be detected.

> **WARNING:** The AEB system is capable of applying heavy braking. Make sure all occupants are safely restrained using seat belts. Unrestrained occupants are at risk of injury and death during heavy braking or impact.

AEB uses cameras and radars pointing forwards at the front of the vehicle to monitor for any imminent collision with road hazards such as:

- Vehicles in front.
- Vehicles in neighboring lanes.
- Pedestrians.
- Cyclists.

AEB aims to reduce the severity of any impact. It may stop the vehicle before an impact takes place, depending on the scenario. If a collision risk is detected, the system brakes the vehicle. The instrument panel displays a warning icon during AEB operation.

##### Automatic Emergency Braking Limitations

Vehicle detection Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) cannot operate if:

- The vehicle is turning a tight corner.
- The AEB cameras are blocked by dirt or other debris.
- Adverse weather conditions (heavy rain, snow, hail etc) prevent the sensors operating.

#### Lane Departure Warning

> **WARNING:** The lane departure warning system is a driving aid only. Responsibility rests with the driver to drive with due care and attention. Always drive in a manner which is safe for the vehicle, its occupants, and other road users. Always observe and respect road signs and road markings.

When the system is enabled, an icon illuminates on the instrument cluster to confirm operation. Lane departure warning may be switched off or on via the Settings - ADAS menu. See [ADAS Menu].

> **NOTE:** When the vehicle is stopped and re-started, the lane departure warning system settings remain as previously set.

The system uses a forward-facing camera, located at the top of the windshield.

> **NOTE:** Make sure the windshield is kept clean and free of obstructions.

An alert is activated if the system detects the vehicle crossing either of the lane markings that it is traveling within, without activation of the appropriate turn signal. The driver is alerted via one of the following methods:

- Steering wheel vibration.
- Red warning icons on the instrument cluster.
- An audible warning tone.

> **NOTE:** The lane departure warning system is a driver warning aid only. The lane departure warning system does not assist in steering the vehicle or operate any of the vehicle's braking systems.

> **NOTE:** The lane departure warning system cannot detect unmarked road edges, worn road markings or temporary traffic markings.

##### Lane Departure Warning limitations

The lane departure warning system relies on sensors and cameras positioned on the vehicle. It is limited in the following conditions: 

- Driving in adverse weather, e.g..heavy fog, rain, snow or hail.
- Driving over worn, damaged, or obscured lane markings, e.g., construction zones.
- Bright lighting.
- In very close proximity to other vehicles.
- Tight turns and sharp gradients.

#### Blind Spot Monitoring

> **WARNING:** The Blind Spot Monitoring system is a driving aid only. Responsibility rests with the driver to change lanes with due care and attention, and to check for hazards all around the vehicle. Always drive in a manner which is safe for the vehicle, its occupants, and other road users. Always carefully check surroundings before attempting any lane change maneuver.

The blind spot monitor system monitors the area beside and approximately 30 ft (10 meters) behind the side mirrors. When the system detects a vehicle is alongside, the warning icon:

- Flashes while a relevant turn signal is being used.
- Lights continuously if the turn signals are off.
- Switches off when the system no longer senses a vehicle in the detection zone.

The blind spot monitor system default mode is ON and the driver cannot switch the system off.

##### Blind Spot Monitor Limitations

The blind spot monitor system does not operate if:

-  **R** (Reverse) or **P** (Park) gear is selected.
- The vehicle's speed is below 5 mph (8 km/h).
- The blind spot monitor sensors become blocked or stop working.
- The side mirrors are damaged.

### PARKING ASSISTANCE


#### Rear View Monitoring

> **WARNING:** The Rear View Monitoring system is a driving aid only. Responsibility rests with the driver to backup with due care and attention, and to check for oncoming hazards. Always drive in a manner which is safe for the vehicle, its occupants, and other road users. Always carefully check surroundings before attempting any reversing maneuver.

Rear Traffic Monitoring checks for cross traffic moving target objects behind the vehicle. While **R** (Reverse) gear is selected, the touchscreen displays an alert icon on the backup camera screen and an audible warning sounds if the vehicle is about to reverse into a hazard. See [Backup Camera].

##### Rear View Monitoring Limitations

The rear view monitor’s function is impaired:

- If the rear view camera is damaged.
- If the Blind Spot Monitor sensors are damaged.
- In poor weather conditions such as snow, ice, heavy rain, hail etc.
- In heavy traffic with many vehicles traveling at differing speeds.
