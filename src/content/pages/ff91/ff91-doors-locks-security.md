### KEYLESS ENTRY


#### Phone as a Key

Phone as a Key uses a smartphone with Bluetooth® for all of the operations that a key fob would normally perform, meaning less devices for you to carry.

You can use your phone for the following functions:

- Remote locking and unlocking
- Passive entry and exit
- Passive starting

The user's phone is programmed when the vehicle is delivered. The first user may program five additional phones by following the instructions shown in [Authenticating a new user in the Faraday Future Mobile App].

#### Removing a Phone Key

To remove a phone key from the vehicle, contact FF Service Support 877-FF-HELP-U. Full contact details for Faraday Future are provided at [Contacting Faraday Future].

#### Using a Phone Key

##### Phone key operation

Once the phone key is within range, the vehicle operates as follows for an authorized user. Approaching the vehicle:

- 30 feet (10 meters) away: vehicle systems wake up. 
- 9 feet (3 meters) away: doors unlock, user is logged into Faraday Future Mobile Application.

Leaving the vehicle:

- 9 feet (3 meters) away: vehicle doors lock automatically (if no other passengers detected).
- 30 feet (10 meters) away: vehicle systems shut down.

##### Phone key troubleshooting

If the vehicle does not respond to a phone key within range:

- Ensure your phone’s battery is charged.
- Ensure that Bluetooth® is enabled in your phone’s settings.
- Try moving the phone as close to your vehicle as possible. Other radio equipment operating on a similar frequency may interfere with the signals from your phone.

If you are still unable to unlock your vehicle with a phone key, use the Faraday Future NFC (Near-Field Communication) card. See [Using the NFC Card]. Contact Faraday Future Service Support if the problem persists.

> **NOTE:** Performance values stated may be affected by environmental conditions that will reduce communication range.

#### Authenticating a new user in the Faraday Future Mobile App

Steps for an authorized user to authenticate prior to using the phone to access the vehicle.

1. Create an FFID (using the Faraday Future app).
2. Confirm that this FFID is authorized by the vehicle’s owner (Gear icon -> User Profiles -> Add Driver). 
 **NOTE:**This step is not required for the vehicle owner.
3. Download the FF CTRL app.
4. Login using the FFID and password. If multiple vehicles are linked to the FFID, be sure to carefully select the desired FF91 on the mobile app to obtain access.
5. Ensure that Bluetooth is turned on using FF CTRL app and Bluetooth permission is given to FF CTRL app.
6. Turn on Location access (Set it to "Always").
7. Approach the vehicle. This triggers the Bluetooth from the mobile device to pair with the vehicle and authenticate the user. 
8. Upon successful authentication, the user will be granted seamless entry access, once the user is within approx. 3 feet (1 meter) of the vehicle. 
9. Click on any door open button to enter the car, once the user is within 9 feet (3 meters) of the vehicle. 
10. Upon exiting and walking away approx. 30 feet (9 meters) from the vehicle, the vehicle will automatically lock itself and sleep. 

A user may also use the FF CTRL app to lock and unlock doors and the liftgate, so long as there is a stable internet connection for both the app and the FF 91 2.0 vehicle.

For every vehicle, the owner's profile and FFID is loaded into the vehicle at time of delivery using the FF CTRL app. The owner may add others as authorized users with the right to enter or exit. These users can use their mobile device as a digital key that allows them to lock or unlock the vehicle via Bluetooth.

#### Using the NFC Card

The Faraday Future NFC card is used within close range of the vehicle only, and is ideal for use as a backup entry device when needed. You might use or lend out the NFC card when:

- Using a valet parking service
-  Scheduling your vehicle to be serviced or repaired by a Faraday Future Service Support Center.
- Experiencing phone key issues (e.g., misplaced phone or low battery)

![](images/ffom0080.png)

Hold the NFC card within 1.5 inches (4 cm) of the sensor on the left B-pillar to lock or unlock the doors.

1. A single tap of the NFC card on the driver’s side B-pillar wakes the car and unlocks it: Accessible Mode.
2. A second tap of the NFC card on the driver’s side B-pillar locks the car and selects Sleep mode.
3. From Drive mode, a tap of the NFC card on the driver’s side B-pillar locks the car and selects Sleep mode.

### DOORS


#### Operating Doors from the Outside

##### Opening doors

The driver’s door automatically opens when:

- A valid phone key is detected within 10 feet (3 meters) of the vehicle.
- A valid phone key or NFC (Near-Field Communication) card is detected touching the sensor on the left B-pillar belt molding.

> **NOTE:** Sensors in the doors can detect obstacles in the door’s opening path. When an obstacle is detected, the door will stop 4 inches (10 cm) away from the obstacle when the obstacle is detected. This applies to both opening and closing doors.

> **NOTE:** The radar works to prevent the doors physically touching objects around the vehicle. If the door is physically touched, it will stop powered movement.

> **NOTE:** In the event of a collision in which airbags are deployed, all door controls become active and the doors are unlocked.

##### Closing doors

All doors automatically close when all valid phone keys leave the 65 feet (20 m) detection radius of the vehicle.

Close a door by manually pushing it until you feel the power cinch motor take over and pull it closed.

When closing, sensors stop the door 10 cm away from a detected obstacle.

#### Child Safety Locks

> **WARNING:** For their safety, the child safety locks should be activated whenever children are seated in the rear seats. There is a risk of death or serious injury if a child operates the rear doors.

> **WARNING:** Never leave children unsupervised in a vehicle.

Your vehicle is equipped with child safety locks on both rear doors. This system prevents occupants from opening the rear doors using the interior door switches.

![](images/ffom0165.png)

Users can activate child lock for rear left, rear right or both rear doors via the Central Information Display. Go to **Settings** and choose **General**. The child lock prevents the selected rear doors from being opened from inside. Windows are not locked as part of child lock. Use the window lock button on the driver’s door to lock passenger window controls. See [Opening and Closing Windows]. 

##### Inhibiting rear window operation

> **WARNING:** Whenever children are seated in the rear seats, the window locks should be activated for their safety. There is a risk of death or serious injury if a child operates the rear windows.

> **WARNING:** Never leave children unsupervised in a vehicle.

![](images/ffom0079.png)

To prevent passengers from operating the rear window switches, the window lock feature can be activated from the switch on the driver’s door. Rear window operation via rear seat switches is disabled when Child Locks are active. See [Child Safety Locks].

The rear window switches are active when the window lock has not been activated.

#### Automatic Locking and Unlocking

When the speed of the vehicle reaches 15 mph (24 km/h), all doors will automatically lock. When the speed falls below 15 mph (24 km/h), they will unlock automatically.

If an event causes the airbags to deploy, once the vehicle speed falls below 15 mph (24 km/h) all doors will automatically unlock but remain latched. See [Effects of Airbag Inflation].

> **NOTE:** In the event of a collision, all door controls become active and the doors are unlocked.

---


#### Opening the Doors with No Power

![](images/ffom0037.png)

If the vehicle has no electrical power, the doors can be opened from the interior by using the mechanical release on the top of the door panel.

Press down on the rear edge of the release to raise the lever. Pull the lever to open the door. 

> **NOTE:** The lever must be pulled twice to open a rear door.

> **NOTE:** Child safety locks will be unavailable in the event of a power loss, even if they were enabled before the vehicle lost power (see [Child Safety Locks]).

If power is not available to open the doors from the outside, use the 12-Volt access point in the front bumper. Contact Faraday Future Service Support for assistance.

 **WARNING:** WARNING: Children, other passengers, or objects can be trapped by the moving windows. Always operate the windows with caution.

- Do not allow children to play with the window switches.
- Never stick objects or body parts through an open window.

Small children and animals are particularly at risk, and should never be left unattended in a vehicle.

### HOOD


#### Hood Opening and Closing

![](images/ffomss0008.png)

When the hood is unlatched, the touchscreen displays a status message.

![Icon Door Ajar Amber-01](images/icon_door_ajar_amber-01.png)  The yellow Door Ajar warning indicator (telltale) is displayed on the Instrument Panel Display (IPD) if a door, the liftgate or the hood is open.

##### Opening the hood

> **WARNING:** Never work on a vehicle that is plugged in. Always remember to unplug the vehicle before working under the hood or underneath the vehicle.

> **NOTE:** All electrical unlatching of the hood is disabled when the vehicle is in motion.

The hood can be unlatched with any of the following methods:

- Use the Faraday Future mobile app.
- On the touchscreen under the **Doors** menu, press the hood icon. See [Controlling Doors Using the Touchscreen].

Once you have successfully unlatched the hood:

![](images/ffom0041.png)

1. Raise the hood.
2. The hood has two gas struts to aid in opening and to hold it in the open position.
3. Ensure the hood is raised to the fully open position.

> **NOTE:** In extremely cold temperatures, you may find that the gas struts do not hold the hood open as firmly as they do in warmer temperatures.

##### Closing the hood

> **WARNING:** Always check the area around the hood for obstructions (such as people or objects) before closing the hood. Failure to do so could result in serious injury to a person or damage to the vehicle.

> **CAUTION:** Do not use excessive force when closing the hood, as the hood panel could be damaged.

1. Gently lower the hood until it is almost closed, then release it.
2. If the weight of the hood does not latch it, the power auto-cinch feature will pull it closed.
3. After closing, check that the latch is fully engaged by attempting to lift the front edge of the hood. The hood should be free from all movement.

### LIFTGATE


#### Liftgate (Trunk) Opening and Closing

> **WARNING:** Be aware of the risk of serious injury when operating the liftgate. Before opening or closing, make sure that no one is in the path of the liftgate who could be injured by its movement.

> **CAUTION:** When operating the liftgate manually, open and close it slowly. If you encounter resistance, do not use force. This could lead to damage and loss of function.

If the liftgate is not fully closed when the vehicle is shifted out of **P** (Park), the system will indicate ‘Liftgate Open’ on the Central Information Display and the Door Ajar telltale will be illuminated on the Instrument Panel Display.

The vehicle must be in **P** (Park) to allow the liftgate to operate.

##### Opening the liftgate

The liftgate can be unlocked and opened by any of the following methods:

![](images/ffom0088.png)

- With a valid phone key within 3 feet (1 meter) of the vehicle, press the switch on the bottom edge of the liftgate.
- On the touchscreen under the **Doors** menu, press the liftgate icon.
- Use the FF CTRL app.

##### Closing the liftgate

To close the liftgate, do one of the following:

- Press the switch on the bottom edge of the liftgate.
- On the touchscreen under the **Doors** menu, press the liftgate icon.
- Use the Faraday Future mobile app.

##### Automatic movement stop

If anything obstructs the liftgate with enough force to prevent it from opening or closing, it will stop moving.

> **NOTE:** The Central Information Display will show a message if any obstacles are detected by the liftgate.

> **NOTE:** If the liftgate stops automatically, the obstruction will also be noted on the touchscreen.

If the liftgate stops automatically due to an obstruction, remove the obstruction and try to open or close it again. If it cannot be opened or closed a second time, try to operate it manually.

##### Liftgate opening angle

![](images/ffom0143.png)

The liftgate opening angle is adjusted via the **Settings** menu on the **General** tab:

1. Currently selected liftgate opening angle.
2. Liftgate opening angle slider. Slide left or right to change the opening angle.

> **NOTE:** The liftgate stops if an obstruction is detected.

### SECURITY SETTINGS


#### Anti-Theft System

##### Alarm system

When the vehicle is locked, the alarm system is automatically armed. A visual and audible alarm will trigger when the system detects any of the following:

- Passenger compartment intrusion (motion within the cabin)
- Tampering with the closures
- Changes in the vehicle tilt, such as during attempts to steal a wheel or tow the vehicle
- Tampering with system components

The vehicle alarm can be armed with the hood open, but cannot be armed with the liftgate open.

##### Alarm activation and deactivation

The alarm is armed when the doors are locked.

The alarm is disarmed when the doors are unlocked. 

##### Arming and disarming the alarm via menu screen

![](images/ffom0123.png)

The alarm can be enabled or disabled using the Central Information Display. The following conditions must be met for the alarm to operate:

1. The vehicle must be in Park.
2. Vehicle speed must be 0 mph.
3. All doors and liftgate must be fully closed.
4. The seats must be unoccupied including the driver’s seat.
5. Tow mode must be OFF.
6. The alarm toggle menu button must be enabled.

##### Immobilizer system

The immobilizer system protects the vehicle from theft by preventing the vehicle from being driven by an unauthorized person. Using the phone key or NFC card disables the immobilizer and allows the vehicle to be driven. See [Starting].

##### Disabling the alarm

You may need to temporarily disable the alarm system in certain circumstances, such as when recovering or transporting the vehicle.
