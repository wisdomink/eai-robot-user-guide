#### Audio Menu

![](images/ffom0174.png)

The audio menu is accessed via the Music icon (11). See [Menu Shortcut Keys]. The audio menu controls source and track options:

1. Radio: Menu key to select the preferred Audio Source. Audio Source options vary depending on what devices are connected to the vehicle.

  - Radio source: A short press seeks upward to the next available frequency.

10. Voice recognition: Press and wait for the audio prompt before speaking a valid command.

### NAVIGATION SYSTEM


#### Setting destinations

To set a destination, press the Navigation shortcut. Enter a destination using the ‘Where would you like to go?’ search bar. Street names, city centres, Zip codes, town names and grid references are all valid entries. 

To navigate directly to this first destination, press Start. To add another waypoint, press ‘Add Stop’ and enter the second destination. Continue to add waypoints until the route is complete. Once ready, press ‘Start’ to begin navigation.

##### Points of interest

Points of interest such as Charging Points, Parking, Restaurants and Groceries are shown under the ‘Where would you like to go?’ search bar. Pressing one of these will bring up a list of the nearest points of interest of the selected type, together with their distance. Selecting one of these displays details such as the address, phone number and website (if applicable). Press ‘Add Stop’ to add to your route. The route will be adjusted to reach the requested point of interest.

##### Map display

Use the compass arrow button in the bottom right side of the map display to switch between North up and Direction up.

##### Manage route

Use the ‘Manage’ gear icon to access a menu to adjust route settings, such as ‘Fastest’, ‘Shortest’ and avoiding toll roads.

##### Head-up display

Route instructions are displayed on the head-up display. See [Head-up Display (HUD)].

##### Traffic data

Traffic information is displayed on the current route as red lines (severe delay) or amber lines (minor delay). If the navigation system detects a quicker route, it prompts the driver to accept the revised route. Traffic and map data is provided by HERE™.

##### Map screen

![](images/ffom0171.png)

The map screen shows the vehicle’s present location. Press and drag to view the surrounding location, or pinch and drag to zoom in/out.

### PHONE


#### Pairing a Phone

To pair a Bluetooth (TM) enabled cellphone or device to the vehicle:

1. Press the ... shortcut on the lower ribbon of the center touchscreen.
2. Access device settings using the gear icon with the solid blue background.
3. Select ‘Connected Devices’. Existing or previous connected devices are displayed on the screen.
4. Select ‘Pair new device’.
5. Using the cellphone or device to be paired, select ‘Pair new device’ from the Bluetooth (TM) menu.
6. Using the cellphone or device to be paired, select ‘FF91’.
7. The cellphone or device will be displayed on the center touchscreen. Press the device’s name to activate the pairing process.
8. The cellphone or device’s name will be displayed in a pop-up dialog box on the screen, together with a message ‘Continue pairing on your device’.
9. A six-digit code is displayed in the dialog box. Confirm the six-digit code matches that displayed on your cellphone or device, and select ‘Pair’ on the device.
10. The device may prompt to ‘Allow Contacts and Favorites Sync’ or similar. FF recommends selecting ‘Allow’ to download your phone contacts and allow full device functionality.
11. Once paired, a message should appear in the notification area to confirm the device has been paired.

Devices with Bluetooth 3.0 and higher may play audio stored on the device via the Music shortcut icon. See [Audio Menu].

### HOMELINK® UNIVERSAL TRANSCEIVER


#### What is HomeLink®?

HomeLink® is a wireless control system that enables you to remotely operate up to 6 Radio Frequency (RF) devices, such as garage doors, gates, lights, and home security systems.

Additional system information can be found online at www.homelink.com.

---


#### HomeLink® FCC/ISED Advisory

This device complies with Federal Communications Commission (FCC) rules part 15 and Innovation, Science, and Economic Development Canada RSS-210. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) This device may not cause harmful interference, and (2) This device must accept any interference that may be received including interference that may cause undesired operation.

WARNING: The transmitter has been tested and complies with FCC and ISED rules. Changes or modifications not expressly approved by the party responsible for compliance could void the user’s authority to operate the device.

This equipment complies with FCC and ISED radiation exposure limits set forth for an uncontrolled environment. End Users must follow the specific operation instructions for satisfying RF exposure compliance. This transmitter must be at least 20 cm from the user and must not be co-located or operating in conjunction with any other antenna or transmitter. 

---


#### Programming HomeLink®

> **WARNING:** Do not use HomeLink® with any garage door opener that cannot detect an object and signal the door to stop and reverse as required by U.S. Federal Safety Standards (including any garage door opener manufactured before April 1st, 1982). A garage door opener which does not have the safety stop and reverse feature does not meet current federal safety standards. Using a garage door opener without these features increases the risk of serious injury or death.

To set up a new HomeLink® connection, have the remote for the device you are programming ready and follow these steps:

![](images/ffom0193.png)

1. Press the Vehicle shortcut.
2. Press **Settings**.
3. Press **HomeLink** menu on left-side of Central Information Display.
4. Press **Add New Device**.
5. Follow the prompts to program a new device.

> **NOTE:** Use the checkbox to toggle auto-open and auto-close chime.

##### Reprogramming a device

1. Follow steps 1-3 above.
2. Press **HomeLink Devices**.
3. Select the required device, then press **Delete**.
4. Press the Back key.
5. Press Add New Device.
6. Follow the prompts to program the device.

 

---

Once devices have been programmed to your vehicle, they are accessed by pressing **Settings** then selecting the **HomeLink** menu from the left side of the Center Information Display.

From this menu:

- Press HomeLink Devices.
- Press a device name to transmit the open/close signal. 

If the Chime is enabled, it sounds to indicate that a signal has been sent to the device.
