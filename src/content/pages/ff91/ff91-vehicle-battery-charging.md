### BATTERY INFORMATION


#### About the Vehicle Batteries

> **WARNING:** Only Faraday Future Authorized Service should service the battery pack. Improper handling can result in death or serious injury.

There are two types of batteries powering your vehicle: a high-voltage lithium-ion battery pack and a 12-Volt lithium-ion battery pack.

> **ENVIRONMENTAL:** Please recycle in accordance with local regulations.

---


#### Battery Pack Care

##### Storage temperature

> **CAUTION:** Avoid exposing your vehicle to ambient temperatures above 104°F (40°C) or below 32°F (0°C). Prolonged exposure can greatly reduce battery pack life and performance.

Extreme ambient temperatures can damage the battery pack. Avoid parking your vehicle in direct sunlight, especially on hot, sunny days. Keep your vehicle sheltered or parked in a garage whenever possible in extremely cold weather.

##### Preserving battery pack health

When your vehicle is left idle and unplugged, the battery pack gradually discharges over time. Allowing the battery pack to completely discharge to 0% could cause permanent damage. Whenever possible, it is recommended to charge your vehicle while it is not in use, especially over long periods of time (such as when in storage). Battery pack life and performance are greatly improved by regular charging.

When the battery pack’s charge level falls below a certain range, warnings will display on the Instrument Cluster to alert you. Go to the nearest charging station as soon as possible in order to avoid a vehicle shutdown.

When the remaining battery pack charge falls below 20%, the Instrument Cluster will change color to alert you that vehicle range and performance is limited.

As is normal with all lithium-ion batteries, the service life and storage capacity of the battery pack will degrade over time. Your vehicle’s systems will alert you when the battery pack needs to be serviced by Faraday Future.

![Icon General-Vehicle-Warning](images/icon_general-vehicle-warning.png)  When poor battery pack health is detected, the warning indicator (telltale) will be displayed on the Instrument Cluster. Contact Faraday Future Service Support as soon as possible.

> **NOTE:** Cold temperatures may reduce the vehicle range. Connect the vehicle to a charger when storing overnight in cold temperatures.

### CHARGING INSTRUCTIONS


#### Safety Checklist

> **WARNING:** If you have any concerns with the condition of a wall outlet, the charge port or the charging cable, DO NOT use them. Seek assistance from a qualified electrician or from Faraday Future Service Support.

Before charging your vehicle, check the following items:

- If using a domestic wall outlet, inspect the outlet and do not use it if it appears damaged or worn.
- Inspect the charging cable and connector for damage, including frays or cracks. If a part appears damaged, do not use it. 
 **NOTE:**If the portable charging cable is damaged, contact Faraday Future Service Support.
- Ensure that the charging cable is fully uncoiled before use.
- Check that the charging connector and charge port are clean and unobstructed. If you find any contamination or a foreign object in either part, do not use them and contact Faraday Future Service Support.
- Check that the charging cable and charge port are dry. Ensure that your hands are dry, and there is no water or other fluids in the surrounding area (such as puddles on the ground).

#### Charge Port Door

The charge port door is located at the rear passenger side and is used for both AC and DC charging. Open the charge port door to access the charging port.

##### Opening the charge port door

> **NOTE:** The doors must be unlocked for the charge port to open.

- Open manually by pressing on the lower left corner of the door
- Select the Energy Window from Central Information Display -> Open Door option.
- Open via the Ctrl app.

> **CAUTION:** If the charge port door has iced over in cold weather conditions, DO NOT attempt to break the ice using blunt force (i.e. hitting or chipping at it with a tool), as this could cause damage.

##### Closing the charge port door

Once a charger is disconnected from the charge port, the charge port door will auto-unlock within one minute. Alternatively, use any of the following methods to close the charge port door:

![](images/ffom0215.png)

- Press the LED-lit button on the left side of the charge port.
- Select the Energy Window from the Central Information Display, then choose the Close Door option.

> **CAUTION:** If the charge port door has iced over in cold weather conditions, DO NOT attempt to break the ice using blunt force (i.e. hitting or chipping at it with a tool), and do not use hot water, as these could cause damage.

> **NOTE:** If the charge port door has iced over, Faraday Future recommends contacting Service Support.

##### Charge port door malfunction

If the charge port door malfunctions, please contact Faraday Future Service Support.

#### Charging the Vehicle

![](images/ffom0020.png)

Plug the charging cable into the vehicle charge port. While charging, the circular LED on the charge port will glow green to show charging is in progress.

> **NOTE:** Clicking or fan noises are normal. During charging, the vehicle system heating and cooling functions turn on or off as needed to help maintain an ideal temperature for the charging system.

##### Errors during charging

When a charging error occurs due to a charging cable or vehicle charging system malfunction, a red LED light will illuminate on the left side of the charge port. The CID may report the fault conditions. If this occurs, remove the charging cable and try again.

If following the steps in the displayed instructions does not resolve the error, contact Faraday Future Service Support for further assistance.

#### Charging Instructions

The vehicle's battery can be charged via the following options:

- Level 1: Slow ‘trickle’ charge rate, sourced from a standard domestic 110v power supply.
- Level 2: Medium charge rate of around 3 - 7 kW per hour. Typically used for overnight charging at home or some public or workplace charging.
- DC charging: Fast charge rate of between 15 - 190 kW per hour. Available at selected public charge stations.

The maximum charging capability of the FF 91 2.0 using AC power is 15.3kW. Maximum charging power depends on the supply voltage as well as charger capability.

> **NOTE:** A 240 volt AC, 64 amp electricity supply is required to achieve 15.3kW charge rate.

The vehicle is supplied with a portable charging cable which can be used either for Level 1 or Level 2 charging. DC charge stations usually incorporate their own charge cable.

 

 

Power output of the portable charge cable:

| Charging Cable Selection | Power |
| --- | --- |
| 15 amp/110 volt | 1.65kW |
| 20 amp/110 volt | 2.20kW |
| 40 amp/240 volt | 9.60kW |

Refer to the instructions provided with the portable charging cable for the best experience.

The vehicle’s DC charge rate varies based on the charging station capability, current vehicle battery State of Charge (SOC), and other factors, up to a maximum of 185kW. The charge rate decreases as the vehicle's SOC approaches 100%.The fastest DC charging occurs below 80% SOC. Above 80% SOC, charge rates are slowed. 

##### Charging Complete or Stop Charging

To end a charging session and remove the charging cable from the vehicle:

- For Level 1 or Level 2 charging, press 'Stop' on the charger, then remove the charging plug from the vehicle charge port.
- For DC charging, use the stop charging function for the DC charging station prior to removing the charging cable from the vehicle.
- Use the FF App to stop charging.

#### Disconnecting the Charging Cable

> **NOTE:** The vehicle cannot be driven when the charging cable is connected to the charge port. If an attached charge cable is detected, the vehicle will not select **D** (Drive) or **R** (Reverse) If you attempt to shift out of **P** (Park).

The charging cable is locked in place during charge events for safety reasons. To remove the cable after charging:

- Ensure that the vehicle is unlocked and that charging has stopped.

![](images/ffom0022.png)

- Press the release button on the charging cable handle for at least two seconds, then pull gently yet firmly.
- Replace the charge port caps. The lower cap must be fitted before the upper cap is replaced.
- After the charging cable has been removed, press the charge port door close button to close the charge port door.

> **CAUTION:** The charge cable can only be released when charging is not in progress.

> **CAUTION:** Prior to closing the charge port door, be sure to fit the AC and DC charge port caps fully to avoid damaging the charge port door.

---


#### Emergency Charging Cable Disconnect

If pressing the button on the charging cable does not release it, make sure the vehicle is unlocked. If the charge cable remains stuck, contact Faraday Future Service Support.

---


#### Charging Status

You can check the progress of the charging process via the light on the charge port or the in-vehicle display.

##### Charge port door LED light colors

The charge port door LED indicates the current charging status. Refer to the following table to understand the meaning of each light color and pattern. The Central Information Display shows charging status including battery SOC, battery voltage, range, energy and charge duration.

| Color | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Blue (solid) | Ready to charge |
| Green (fades in and out) | Charging in progress |
| Green (solid) | Charging completed |
| Red (solid) | Charging error |
