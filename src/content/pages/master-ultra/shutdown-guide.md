# Shutdown Guide

## Hoisted Shutdown (Gantry Needed)

Step 1: Install the Hoisting Ring and Perform Initial Hoisting
- Install the hoisting ring on the back of the robot and ensure it is securely attached.
- Use the electric gantry hoist to lift the robot to an initial hoisting position, preparing for
the subsequent mode switching operation.

![Step1: Install the lifting rings](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-1.png)

Step 2: Switch to Standing Preparation Mode (Position-Controlled Standing) Safety Reminder: Before switching modes, do not lift both feet of the robot completely off the ground to prevent potential equipment damage or personal injury.

Operating Steps:
1. Keep the robot in the hoisted position and slightly raise the lifting rope to prevent sudden forward tilting.
2. Use the remote controller and press [L2 + X] simultaneously to switch to Standing Preparation Mode (Position-Controlled Standing).

![Step2: Use the remote controller to switch the robot to the standing preparation mode](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-2.png)

Step 3: Enter Zero-Torque Mode

Recommended Safe Procedure:
- First switch to Damping Mode: short press [L2 + R2 + Create].
- Then switch to Zero-Torque Mode: short press [L1 + R1 + Create].

Emergency Stop:
If an immediate stop is required, you can directly enter Zero-Torque Mode via the remote controller.

![Step3: Use the remote controller to switch the robot to the zero-torque mode](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-3.png)

Step 4: Hoist the Robot After confirming that the robot has entered Zero-Torque Mode, use the electric gantry hoist to slowly lift the robot until both feet are completely off the ground.

![Step4: Robot fully hoisted, both feet off the ground](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-4.png)

Step 5: Power Off
- Short press the power button on the back of the battery to wake the device, then long
press for about 5 seconds to power off.
- During shutdown, LEDs 1–4 on the battery will turn off sequentially, indicating
successful power-off.

![Step5: Press the power button to turn off the robot](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-5.png)

Step 6: Repack the Robot
- Open the side door of the packaging box and ensure enough space for placement.
- Following the illustration, first place the legs into the bottom positioning slots, then
fold the upper body into the box.
- Confirm the robot is securely positioned before closing and locking the packaging box.

![Step6-1: Place the legs into the positioning slots as shown.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-6.png)

![Step6-2: Fold the upper body into the box to complete packing.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-hoisted-7.png)

## Assisted Lying-Down Shutdown (No Gantry Required)

Note:
After switching the robot to standing-preparation (position-controlled standing) mode and before it fully lies down, one or two operators should assist throughout the process to ensure the robot lands smoothly and remains balanced.

Step 1: Enter Preparation (Position-Controlled Standing) Mode
- Use the remote controller and short press [L2 + X] simultaneously to switch the robot
to standing-preparation (position-controlled standing) mode.
- During operation, manually support the robot from behind using the back handle to
prevent tipping caused by imbalance.

![Step1: Switch to Standing-Preparation Mode (Position-Controlled Standing)](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-1.png)

Step 2: Lay the Robot Down
- Gently lower the robot from the standing-preparation posture to a Prone Position on
the ground, ensuring smooth and controlled movement.
- It is recommended that one or two operators assist to prevent tipping or abnormal
postures during placement.

![Step2: Gently Lay the Robot Flat](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-2.png)

Step 3: Enter Zero-Torque Mode
- After confirming that the robot is fully lying flat and in contact with the ground, use the
remote controller to change modes.
- Avoid operating the controller before the robot is stable to prevent accidental falls.

Recommended Safe Procedure:
- First switch to Damping Mode: short press [L2 + R2 + Create].
- Then switch to Zero-Torque Mode: short press [L1 + R1 + Create].

Emergency Stop:
If an immediate stop is required, you can directly enter Zero-Torque Mode via the remote controller.

![Step3: Switch to Zero-Torque Mode After the Robot is Fully Flat](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-3.png)

Step 4: Power Off
- Short press the power button on the back of the battery to wake the device, then long
press for about 5 seconds to power off.
- During shutdown, LEDs 1–4 on the battery will turn off sequentially, indicating
successful power-off.

![Step4: Press the power button to turn off the robot](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-4.png)

Step 5: Repack the Robot
- Open the side door of the packaging box and ensure enough space for placement.
- Following the illustration, first place the legs into the bottom positioning slots, then
fold the upper body into the box.
- Confirm the robot is securely positioned before closing and locking the packaging box.

![Step5-1: Place the legs into the positioning slots as shown.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-5.png)

![Step5-2: Fold the upper body into the box to complete packing.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-lyingdown-6.png)

## Sitting-Position Shutdown (No Gantry Required)

Step 1: Command the Robot to Sit Down
- Use the remote controller and short press [L2 + ←] simultaneously to make the robot
perform the sitting motion.
- Place the robot on a stable platform or bench approximately 35–40 cm high, ensuring
firm support.

![Step 1: Use the remote controller to make the robot perform the sitting motion.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-sitting-1.png)

Step 2: Enter Zero-Torque Mode
- Once the robot is in a stable sitting posture facing forward, use the remote controller
to switch to zero-torque mode to ensure safe shutdown.
- During the transition, manually support the robot from behind using the back handle or
ensure its back rests firmly against the chair to maintain balance and prevent falling.

Recommended Safe Procedure:
- First switch to Damping Mode: short press [L2 + R2 + Create].
- Then switch to Zero-Torque Mode: short press [L1 + R1 + Create].

Emergency Stop:
If an immediate stop is required, directly enter zero-torque mode using the remote controller.

Step 3: Power Off
- Short press the power button on the back of the battery to wake the device, then long
press for about 5 seconds to power off.
- During shutdown, LEDs 1–4 on the battery will turn off sequentially, indicating
successful power-off.

![Step 3: Press the power button to turn off the robot](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-sitting-2.png)

Step 4: Repack the Robot
- Open the side door of the packaging box and ensure enough space for placement.
- Following the illustration, first place the legs into the bottom positioning slots, then
fold the upper body into the box.
- Confirm the robot is securely positioned before closing and locking the packaging box.

![Step4-1: Place the legs into the positioning slots as shown.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-sitting-3.png)

![Step4-2: Fold the upper body into the box to complete packing.](/images/master-ultra/shutdown-sitting-4.png)

Sitting-Position Shutdown Notes:
- During the sitting motion, ensure the robot is placed stably on a 35–40 cm
high bench or platform with a backrest for support.
- When switching from sitting posture to zero-torque mode, manually support
the back handle or keep the robot leaning against the backrest to maintain balance and prevent tipping.
