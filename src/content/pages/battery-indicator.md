# Battery Indicator Lights

> **⚠️ Important Note:** When the robot is powered by the battery, please **monitor** the battery level carefully. If the battery power is low or nearly depleted, the robot may lose power suddenly and collapse. When only one indicator light remains on, charge or replace the battery promptly to ensure stable operation and prevent potential injury or damage caused by the robot falling due to power loss.

## Battery Level Indicators

| Indicator | Battery Level | Status |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| 4 lights on | 75% – 100% | Full |
| 3 lights on | 50% – 75% | Good |
| 2 lights on | 25% – 50% | Low |
| 1 light on | 0% – 25% | Critical – Charge immediately |
