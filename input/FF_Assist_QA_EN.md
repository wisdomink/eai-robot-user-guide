## FF 91 2.0 Futurist

**Path:** `/product/ff-91-Futurist`  
**Greeting:** Want to learn about the FF 91 2.0?

### 2.1 What is the range and charging time of the FF 91 2.0?

The FF 91 2.0 is equipped with a **142.3 kWh** high-voltage battery pack. Range and charging time vary depending on battery state, charger output, and environmental conditions.

**Range**
- The instrument cluster displays range in several alert thresholds:
 - **Over 50 miles (80 km):** Normal display
 - **50–11 miles (80–18 km):** Prompt to charge soon
 - **10 miles (16 km) or below:** Prompt to stop and charge immediately
- When the state of charge drops below approximately **20%**, the instrument enters a warning state and vehicle performance is limited to extend range.

**Charging Time**
- **AC charging (Level 2):** Maximum charging capacity is **15.3 kW**, requiring a **240V / 64A** power source.
- **DC fast charging:** Up to **185 kW**; fastest below **80% SOC** — charging rate tapers above 80%.
- Portable charging cable approximate output:
 - **110V / 15A: 1.65 kW**
 - **110V / 20A: 2.20 kW**
 - **240V / 40A: 9.60 kW**

I can also estimate full-charge times for home Level 1/Level 2 vs. public DC fast charging — just let me know.

### 2.2 What intelligent driving features are supported?

The FF 91 2.0 supports the following ADAS / intelligent driving assistance features:

| Feature | Function |
|---|---|
| **AEB – Automatic Emergency Braking** | Detects potential forward collision risk and automatically applies braking to mitigate impact |
| **FCW – Forward Collision Warning** | Issues audible and visual alerts when a potential collision scenario is detected |
| **LDW – Lane Departure Warning** | Alerts you when the vehicle unintentionally drifts out of the lane |
| **LKA – Lane Keeping Assist** | Provides steering corrections to help maintain lane position |
| **TSR – Traffic Sign Recognition** | Recognizes speed limit signs, no-passing signs, etc., and displays them on the instrument cluster |
| **ACC – Adaptive Cruise Control** | Automatically maintains set speed and following distance; brings the vehicle to a full stop if the vehicle ahead stops |
| **BSM – Blind Spot Monitoring** | Monitors rear-side blind zones and alerts you to lane-change risks |
| **Rear Cross-Traffic Alert (RCTA)** | Detects crossing traffic or moving objects behind the vehicle while reversing |
| **High Beam Auto-Dimming** | Automatically switches between high and low beams based on ambient light and oncoming traffic |

Please note: all of these are **driver assistance** features — they are not autonomous driving systems that replace the driver. You remain fully responsible for vehicle control and safety at all times.

## aiHyper 5x4 Architecture

**Path:** `/aihyper-architecture`  
**Greeting:** Want to learn about FF's technology platform?

### 11.1 What does the 5x4 architecture include?

The **FF aiHyper 5x4 Architecture** is FF's full-stack Embodied AI technology platform, comprising **5 Core Platforms** and **4 Vertical Technology Systems**.

Both FF EAI vehicles and FF EAI robots share the same **5 Core EAI Platforms**, each with their own independent **4 Vertical Technology Systems**:

1. **EAI Skills Open Platform** — A cross-form-factor skill ecosystem platform enabling embodied AI agents to create, orchestrate, share, and scale executable capabilities
2. **EAI Brain Platform** — A multi-form-factor universal AI brain, evolving from manipulation autonomy to task autonomy and toward true general autonomy
3. **EAI Cloud Training Platform** — A unified cloud and data platform supporting large-scale deployment, continuous learning, and safe lifecycle management of embodied AI agents
4. **EAI HW Platform** — A standardized electronics and computing platform integrating perception, communication, power, and compute to build a reliable EAI foundation
5. **EAI Mechanical Platform** — A modular mechanical and actuation platform delivering precise motion, structural integrity, and cross-form-factor scalable embodied execution

**FF EAI Vehicle — 4 Vertical Technology Systems:**
- EAI Body: body structure, chassis frame, underbody systems
- EAI P&C: powertrain, battery, suspension, braking, steering
- EAI Space: smart cabin, connected applications, intelligent connectivity
- EAI Driving: autonomous driving system

**FF EAI Robot — 4 Vertical Technology Systems:**
- EAI Body & Structure: The robot's "skeleton and skin" — manages body structure, form factor design, and module integration; ensures strength, stability, and protection; provides standardized mounting and interface foundations for actuators, sensors, compute, and energy.
- EAI Driving Force & Energy: The robot's "heart, blood, and muscles" — manages joint actuation, power output, and energy management; ensures continuous and stable physical output across varying task intensities through power control, thermal management, and safety protection.
- EAI Intellectual & Interactive: The robot's "soul" — manages information understanding and expression between humans and the environment; serves as the unified entry point for all non-physical interaction; converts human commands and context into interpretable semantic inputs and feeds back the robot's state, intent, and results.
- EAI Locomotion & Manipulation: The robot's "cerebellum and neural center" — translates task intent into executable actions; handles full-body motion, manipulation, and real-time control; forms a closed-loop autonomous capability from execution and monitoring through feedback and recovery.

For a deeper look at any subsystem (e.g., aiDriving sensors, aiPowertrain specs), feel free to ask.

**aiDriving** is the intelligent driving system within the aiHyper architecture. Using the **FF 91 2.0** as reference, the sensor suite includes:

FX vehicles (Super One, etc.) will feature upgraded sensor configurations on the same architecture. Refer to individual vehicle pages for specifics.

### 11.3 How is it different from other automakers' platforms?

**aiHyper**'s core differentiators from traditional OEM platforms:

| Dimension | Traditional OEM Platform | FF aiHyper |
|---|---|---|
| Software Architecture | Distributed ECUs, isolated functions | Central domain controller + SDV, continuous OTA evolution |
| AI Role | Add-on feature | AI-native, embedded across driving / cabin / powertrain / safety |
| Cross-product Sharing | Independent development per brand | FF and FX share the same underlying architecture |
| Ecosystem | Closed | Open — interconnected with EAI robots |

In short, aiHyper is an **AI-native + cross-product-line shared + continuously evolving** technology platform — not just a chassis, but the foundation of FF's entire intelligent product ecosystem.
