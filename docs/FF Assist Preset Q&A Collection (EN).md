### 2.1 What is the range and charging time of the FF 91 2.0?

The FF 91 2.0 is equipped with a **142.3 kWh** high-voltage battery. Range and charging time vary with state of charge, charger power, and environmental conditions.

**Range**
- The instrument cluster displays range in several alert zones:
  - **Over 50 miles (80 km)**: Normal display
  - **50–11 miles (80–18 km)**: Prompt to charge soon
  - **10 miles (16 km) and below**: Prompt to stop and charge immediately
- When battery drops below approximately **20%**, the instrument cluster enters a warning state and vehicle performance is limited to extend range.

**Charging Time**
- **AC charging**: Maximum charging capacity is **15.3 kW**, requiring a **240V / 64A** power supply.
- **DC fast charging**: Up to **185 kW**, fastest below **80% SOC**; charging speed tapers off above **80%**.
- Approximate power for portable chargers:
  - **110V / 15A: 1.65 kW**
  - **110V / 20A: 2.20 kW**
  - **240V / 40A: 9.60 kW**

If you'd like, I can also estimate rough charge times for "home slow charging / public fast charging" separately.

### 2.2 What smart driving features are supported?

The FF 91 2.0 supports the following intelligent driving / driver assistance features:

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **AEB – Automatic Emergency Braking** | Detects potential frontal collision risks and automatically brakes when necessary to mitigate impact |
| **FCW – Forward Collision Warning** | Audible and visual alert when a potential collision scenario is detected |
| **LDW – Lane Departure Warning** | Alerts you when the vehicle unintentionally drifts out of a lane |
| **LKA – Lane Keeping Assist** | Provides steering corrections to help maintain lane position |
| **TSR – Traffic Sign Recognition** | Reads speed limits, no-overtaking signs, etc. and displays them on the dashboard |
| **ACC – Adaptive Cruise Control** | Automatically follows traffic and maintains following distance at a set speed; can bring the vehicle to a full stop |
| **BSM – Blind Spot Monitoring** | Monitors the rear side blind zones and warns of lane-change risks |
| **Rear View Monitoring** | Detects crossing traffic or moving objects behind the vehicle while reversing |
| **High Beam Auto-Dimming** | Automatically switches between high and low beams based on environment and oncoming lights |

Note: These are all **driver assistance** features, not autonomous driving systems that replace the driver. You remain responsible for vehicle control and safety at all times.

### 13.2 What should I do if my robot won't turn on?

If your robot won't turn on, follow these quick troubleshooting steps:

1. **Check the battery**
   - Is the battery properly inserted?
   - Does it have at least **2 bars of charge**? Low battery can prevent startup.
2. **Confirm the startup method**
   - First **short-press** the power button to wake the device
   - Then **long-press for approximately 5 seconds** to complete startup
3. **Check the charger**
   - Must use the **official power adapter** (not third-party)
   - Adapter and power cable must be undamaged
4. **Observe status indicators**
   - Are any indicator lights on? What color?
   - Is there a beep sound?
5. **Check for anomalies**
   - Has the unit been exposed to water, dropped, or had the battery replaced recently?
